# events

What is on: dated happenings — a market day, a tasting, a concert night — each row led by its date as the business says it, then its time, its name, a line about it and a picture, with those already over left off by the site.

## Placement

- carte.offer — fits: the list the page is for, in the widest column, the date beside each name
- carte.main — fits: under the offer when the offer holds the menu or the catalogue
- marketing.main — fits: across the reading width, the date beside the name and above it in a narrow column
- split.main — fits: under the opening's two halves
- poster.main — fits: the next few, below the fold, since a long list is carte's
- board.main — fits: a tile of its own, the date above the name in a third of the page
- journal.main — fits: what is on, in the narrow column, the date above the name
- stacked.main — fits: one card of the grid, a third of the width
- focus.main — fits: the dates the one task is about, in the 36rem column
- console.main — fits: the admin's view of what a visitor reads, in its column of data
- workspace.detail — fits: one block of the chosen item
- * — never: nothing wide goes in a side column, an opening says what the page is and a band asks for one thing

## Limits

- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)
- events — 12; advice 8 (choice-overload)
- per-page — 1 (choice-overload)

## Behaviour

- It lives on the one page whose role is what is on, and home links to that page, from a feature item or the hero's second link, rather than listing the dates again. (choice-overload, hicks-law)
- A bare tag draws the events the owner keeps in the admin, those already over left off on the site's own clock, and dates written on the tag are the brief's own: never an invented event with a real date. (teslers-law, cognitive-bias)
- Every row leads with its date as the business says it, samedi 12 octobre, the machine's date kept on the time element, so the list reads in the order a visitor plans in. (serial-position-effect, postels-law)
- A heading names it when the page's h1 does not already: the agenda's title on its own page, a section above it anywhere else. (chunking)
- Linked from the navigation as #agenda, it carries id="agenda" on its own tag, or the link lands nowhere. (flow, peak-end-rule)
