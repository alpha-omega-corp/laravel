# card-heading

The band across the top of an app's panel: the panel's title, one line about it, and the action on what the panel holds, over the rule that divides it from the body.

## Placement

- console.main — fits: the band on top of the panel it heads, a description list or a list
- workspace.list — first: the list's title band at the top of its 20rem column
- workspace.detail — fits: the band on top of one panel of the chosen item
- * — never: it heads an app's panel, while a site's heading is a section's title, which a direction draws, and a card of a grid or a task titles itself

## Limits

- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)
- description — 80 characters; advice 40 (cognitive-load)
- actions — 2 (hicks-law)

## Behaviour

- It heads the panel it sits on, the description list or the list straight under it, and never stands alone. (law-of-common-region, law-of-proximity)
- Its description is one line cut at its width, about 40 characters in workspace's 20rem list and 80 in console's main, so it is a count or a status rather than a sentence. (cognitive-load)
- Its actions act on what the panel holds, at the band's right end and one at most in workspace's list, while the screen's one primary stays the page-heading's. (law-of-proximity, von-restorff-effect)
- One screen heads its panels one way, card-heading or section-heading, never both for panels of the same kind. (law-of-similarity)
