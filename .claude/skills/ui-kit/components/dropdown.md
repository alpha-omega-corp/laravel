# dropdown

A button that opens a short menu of links under it, such as a row's actions or the account's menu, the opening and closing left to the browser.

## Placement

- * — never: it opens over the page from a button inside what it acts on, a page-heading's actions or a table's row, never as a region's own component; and never on a public page, where a visitor would meet a menu hidden behind a button

## Limits

- items — 9; advice 5 (hicks-law, millers-law)
- label — 24 characters (cognitive-load)

## Behaviour

- It holds the secondary actions of the thing it sits in, such as a row's edit and archive or the account's settings; the screen's primary action stays a button in view. (von-restorff-effect, hicks-law)
- Its label says what the menu is about, such as the account's name or Export, since its items are only seen after the click. (mental-model)
- A divider separates groups of items, a handful each. (chunking, law-of-proximity)
- Every item has an href: one without is a # that leads nowhere. (peak-end-rule)
