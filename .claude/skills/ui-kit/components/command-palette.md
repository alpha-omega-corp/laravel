# command-palette

A search over an app's screens and commands, typed into and chosen from, each match with its shortcut: shown open here, a dialog everywhere else.

## Placement

- * — never: it opens from the keyboard over an app screen, never as a region's own component; and never on a public page, whose visitors find their way by its navigation

## Limits

- per-page — 1 (cognitive-load)

## Behaviour

- It is a shortcut to what the navigation already offers, never the only way to a screen or a command. (paradox-of-the-active-user, jakobs-law)
- Its placeholder says what it searches, since it is also the field's only name. (paradox-of-the-active-user)
- Its empty text says what to try when nothing matches; left out, a search that finds nothing shows a blank panel. (flow)
- The best match comes first, and each shows its shortcut as its hint, so the palette also teaches the keys. (serial-position-effect, paradox-of-the-active-user)
- It opens on the keys people already know, Ctrl or Cmd with K, and closes on Escape. (jakobs-law)
