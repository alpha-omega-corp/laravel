# process

How it goes, in the order it goes: numbered steps from the first call to the handover, each with its name, one line and how long it takes.

## Placement

- marketing.main — fits: across the reading width, as many steps across as fit at 14rem and one under another on a phone
- split.main — fits: under the opening's two halves
- carte.main — fits: under the offer, how one of those is ordered or carried out
- poster.main — fits: below the fold, three or four steps in a row
- board.main — fits: a tile of its own, the steps one under another in a third of the page
- journal.story — fits: the method told as part of the story, the steps one under another in the narrow column
- journal.main — fits: the practical part of the narrow column, the steps one under another
- stacked.main — fits: one card of the grid, the steps one under another
- focus.main — fits: the road ahead of the one task, in the 36rem column
- * — never: steps are read in order across a width a side column does not have, an opening says what the page is, and a band asks for one thing rather than explaining it

## Limits

- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)
- intro — 160 characters (cognitive-load)
- steps — 7; advice 5 (millers-law, chunking)
- per-page — 1 (occams-razor)

## Behaviour

- It stands just above the page's ask, the quote form or the band, because a visitor who has seen the road commits to its first step. (goal-gradient-effect, zeigarnik-effect)
- The steps are the business's own way of working, in its order and its words, and never four generic ones, contact, analysis, execution, delivery, written to fill the row. (occams-razor, cognitive-bias)
- Each step is a name and one line, and its number is written, so a visitor can say “we are at step three” to the person on the phone. (chunking, mental-model)
- A duration is the owner's, deux semaines or le jour même, and is left out rather than promised for them. (cognitive-bias, postels-law)
- The first step is what the visitor does next and the last is what they are left with, since those two are the ones remembered. (serial-position-effect, peak-end-rule)
- Linked from the navigation as #deroulement, it carries id="deroulement" on its own tag, or the link lands nowhere. (flow, peak-end-rule)
- Its arrangement is chosen on its tag and the direction's look stays: `variant="timeline"` runs the steps down one rule, for a road with unequal stages; `variant="row"` joins them across the column with a line, for three or four of equal weight; bare, it is the direction's own. (law-of-uniform-connectedness, goal-gradient-effect)
