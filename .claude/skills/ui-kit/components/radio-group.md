# radio-group

One answer out of a few, every option in view, such as a party size, a delivery or a plan: dots beside their labels, or cards when each option needs a line to choose by.

## Placement

- focus.main — fits: the step's one choice, its button straight after it; cards run the width of the 36rem column
- * — never: a radio group is a field of a form and goes inside its form-layout

## Limits

- options — 9; advice 5 (hicks-law, millers-law)

## Behaviour

- It always has a legend, the question its options answer; without one they answer nothing a screen reader can say. (paradox-of-the-active-user)
- The usual answer is selected when there is one, so the common case is one click, but never a paid extra and never the option the business would rather sell. (parkinsons-law, cognitive-bias)
- Past five options, or when the options need no weighing, a select says the same in less room. (hicks-law, choice-overload)
- Cards when each option needs a line to choose by, such as a delivery and its price; the chosen card shows it by its edge and its ground as well as its dot. (law-of-common-region, von-restorff-effect)
