# hero

The opening of a page: its name, what it is and the one thing to do, beside the photograph that says it — or, until there is one, the frame that says which photograph is owed.

## Placement

- marketing.hero — first: across the full width
- split.hero — first: 7fr of the opening from lg while visit holds something and the whole width when it is empty; its copy and photograph sit side by side only while its own column holds two 24rem halves, and stack on a phone
- poster.hero — first: the cover, one screen tall, with the facts along its foot
- board.hero — first: a short opening above the board, where the recipe may put a section at level 1 instead
- journal.hero — first: the lead photograph and the title, across the page
- carte.lead — never: carte opens on its list, and its heading is a section at level 1
- * — never: a page has one opening, and an app screen opens on its page-heading

## Limits

- links — 2; advice 1 (hicks-law, von-restorff-effect)
- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz, cognitive-load)
- lead — 160 characters (cognitive-load)
- facts — 3 (chunking)
- per-page — 1 (cognitive-load)

## Behaviour

- It opens the page, and nothing in its region comes before it: no alert, badge or promotion ahead of what the page is. (selective-attention, serial-position-effect)
- Its title is the page's one h1; on a page other than home it names that page, in the words of its navigation label. (chunking, mental-model)
- Its first link is the page's primary action and goes where the header's action goes, so the first screen has one primary target; a second link is a secondary button. (von-restorff-effect, hicks-law)
- It carries a photograph, or the subject of the one that is owed, and never an empty frame. (aesthetic-usability-effect, peak-end-rule)
- Its photograph is the page's one eager image, and every other picture on the page loads lazily. (doherty-threshold)
- The facts, the note and the lead say only what the brief states: an invented figure on a business's page is a false claim. (cognitive-bias)
