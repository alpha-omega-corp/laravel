# donate

How to give: a sentence on what a gift does, one button that opens the association's own giving page, and the account for those who give by transfer, never a till.

## Placement

- marketing.main — fits: in the reading width, on the page whose role is to support, the account beside the words from sm
- split.main — fits: under the opening's two halves
- carte.main — fits: under the offer, for the visitor who wants to help what they have just read about
- poster.main — fits: below the fold, after the cover has said what the association does
- board.main — fits: a tile of its own, the account under the words in a third of the page
- journal.story — fits: the ask at the end of the story, in the narrow column
- journal.main — fits: the practical part of the narrow column
- stacked.main — fits: one card of the grid, a third of the width
- focus.main — fits: the one task of the page, in the 36rem column
- * — never: a side column is for the hours and the way in, an opening says what the page is, and a band is the cta-band's, which may lead here

## Behaviour

- It lives on the one page whose role is to support, and the header's action or the band leads there from every page, rather than an ask on each. (hicks-law, von-restorff-effect)
- It never takes a card, an amount or a name: the button opens the giving page the association already uses, and a site that must charge is given a checkout by deployer's /stripe. (mental-model, jakobs-law)
- The account is the owner's, copied whole, the holder, the IBAN and the reference, in even figures and selectable in one click, and is never retyped or completed from memory. (postels-law, cognitive-bias)
- What a gift may claim, a tax deduction, a recognised status, a label, is said only in the association's own words and is never written for it. (cognitive-bias, teslers-law)
- The sentence above the button says what one gift does, in a thing a donor can picture, une saison de matériel pour une équipe, rather than thanking them in advance. (mental-model, peak-end-rule)
- It asks once, plainly, and nothing on the page counts down, pulses or pleads beside it. (selective-attention, aesthetic-usability-effect)
- Linked from the navigation as #donate, it carries id="donate" on its own tag, or the link lands nowhere. (flow, peak-end-rule)
- Its arrangement is chosen on its tag and the direction's look stays: `variant="split"` sets the words and the button on one side and the account on the other, in main at its width; bare, it is the direction's own. (law-of-common-region, law-of-proximity)
