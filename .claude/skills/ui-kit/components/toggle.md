# toggle

A switch for a setting that is on or off, such as showing the prices or opening on Sundays, drawn by its label over a checkbox, so it works without JavaScript and is sent with its form.

## Placement

- focus.main — fits: one setting of the step, above its button
- * — never: a toggle is a field of a form and goes inside its form-layout, above all the admin's settings

## Behaviour

- It is a setting, named by what it turns on, such as Show the prices; a question, a consent or a public form's choice is a checkbox. (jakobs-law, mental-model)
- Flipped, it changes nothing until its form is sent, so it sits in a form-layout beside its save and never on a screen that has none. (mental-model)
- It is never on by default for what nobody chose, such as a newsletter, a paid extra or a notice sent to the owner's customers. (cognitive-bias)
- Its label is always given: the switch itself is a label with no words, so the text beside it is its only name. (paradox-of-the-active-user)
- Each has a name of its own, which is also its id, so its label and its track flip it and no other. (fittss-law)
