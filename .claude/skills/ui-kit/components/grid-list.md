# grid-list

People or things of one kind as cards in a grid, each with its initial, its name and one line: who or what is there, at a glance.

## Placement

- console.main — fits: the screen's items as cards, two across from sm and three from lg beside the 18rem sidebar
- workspace.detail — fits: the chosen item's people or parts, columns 2, since the rail and the list take 24rem
- * — never: nothing wide goes in a side column, a card or a 36rem task, and on a site people and things are features or the catalogue, which a direction draws

## Limits

- items — 12 (choice-overload)

## Behaviour

- Its avatar draws the first letter of each title, so a title opens on the name, never on a number or a symbol. (mental-model, law-of-similarity)
- Every card holds the same kind of thing with the same fields; a mixed set is a stacked-list. (law-of-similarity)
- Its names are cut to one line, so each is the short name people use for it. (cognitive-load)
- Past twelve cards it is paged, or grouped under section-headings by what the cards share. (choice-overload, chunking)
