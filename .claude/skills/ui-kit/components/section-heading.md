# section-heading

The heading over one section of an app screen: its title above a rule, a line about it, and the actions or the tabs that act on that section alone.

## Placement

- console.main — fits: over the section it heads, a feed or a list, across main's one column
- workspace.list — first: the list's title at the top of its 20rem column, above the filter
- workspace.detail — fits: over one part of the chosen item, under its page-heading
- * — never: a site's section heads itself with its own title, the h2 a direction draws, and a card, a task or a title strip has no second heading

## Limits

- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)
- description — 160 characters (cognitive-load)
- actions — 2 (hicks-law)

## Behaviour

- It heads the one section straight under it, a feed, a list or a table, and never stands with nothing under it. (law-of-proximity, chunking)
- Its actions and its tabs act on that section alone, as secondary buttons, since the screen's one primary is the page-heading's. (von-restorff-effect, law-of-proximity)
- Its description says in a line what the section holds; help for a field is that field's hint, never a paragraph here. (paradox-of-the-active-user, cognitive-load)
- One screen heads its sections one way, section-heading or card-heading, never both for sections of the same kind. (law-of-similarity)
