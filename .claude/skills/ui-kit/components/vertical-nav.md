# vertical-nav

Rows of navigation with no panel of their own, for a column that already has a surface: a sidebar's screens, the one shown marked by a fill or by a rule down its edge.

## Placement

- console.nav — first: rows down the 18rem sidebar, flush with its edge; on a phone a band of rows above the page
- * — never: a sidebar's rows; a site's bar is its site-header, and one column of data takes no second navigation

## Limits

- items — 9; advice 5 (hicks-law, millers-law)

## Behaviour

- The screen being shown is given current, since nothing else marks it, and the mark is a fill or a rule as well as the accent, never the colour alone. (working-memory, von-restorff-effect)
- It is the same on every screen it serves, the same rows in the same order. (jakobs-law, law-of-similarity)
- Every row has an href: one without is a # that leads nowhere. (peak-end-rule)
- A label is a word or two and at most 16 characters, since a longer one is cut short with an ellipsis. (cognitive-load)
- A badge is a count to act on, such as unread messages, never a label's decoration. (selective-attention)
