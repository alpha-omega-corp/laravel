# features

A section of blocks under one heading, the rooms, the products, the services or what the place is known for, each block a photograph, a title and a few words.

## Placement

- marketing.main — fits: its items side by side while each holds its photograph and its words at 16rem, one under another on a phone
- split.main — fits: under the opening's two halves
- carte.main — fits: under the list and its hours
- poster.main — fits: below the fold and plain, the cover being the page's loud part
- board.main — fits: one tile or the first 2×2 one, each item putting its photograph beside its words only while the tile holds both
- journal.gallery — fits: the one band of pictures across the page, breaking the column once
- journal.* — never: the column is the story's words, and a grid of blocks in it turns the magazine back into a landing page
- carte.offer — never: the offer is the list, a menu or a catalogue
- * — never: nothing wide goes in a side column, an opening is the hero's and a band is the page's ask, and an app screen is not a site's

## Limits

- items — 9; advice 6 (millers-law, chunking)
- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)
- lead — 160 characters (cognitive-load)
- per-page — 1 (law-of-similarity)

## Behaviour

- Each item is a title and at most two sentences, with a photograph or the subject of the one owed: a block is scanned, not read. (chunking, cognitive-load)
- Its items are what the brief names, such as the rooms, the products or the services, and never three equal cards made up to fill the middle. (occams-razor, cognitive-bias)
- On home, an item that stands for another page links to it, so home says a little of each and the page says the rest. (hicks-law, pareto-principle)
- An item's link is labelled with what it opens, never “read more” or “en savoir plus”. (paradox-of-the-active-user)
- An icon goes above an item's title for a service or a line of products, and never takes the place of its photograph. (cognitive-load, law-of-pragnanz)
- It is the page's one block of this shape, and a second set of blocks is a second shape instead: a gallery, a quote, a short list. (law-of-similarity)
- Linked from the navigation as #la-ferme, it carries id="la-ferme" on its own tag, or the link lands nowhere. (flow, peak-end-rule)
