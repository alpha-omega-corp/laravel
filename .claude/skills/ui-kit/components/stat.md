# stat

One figure with its label and how it changed: a dashboard's key number, three of them in a row across the top of the grid.

## Placement

- stacked.main — fits: the first cards of the grid, three in a row from lg, two from md and one on a phone
- * — never: console's main is one column where nothing sits beside anything, and on a site a figure is one of the hero's facts, and only one the brief states

## Limits

- value — 6 characters (law-of-pragnanz, cognitive-load)
- per-page — 6; advice 3 (pareto-principle, chunking)

## Behaviour

- Its figure is six characters at most, abbreviated such as 12.4k with the unit in the label, since beside its change a longer one runs out of a third-width card. (law-of-pragnanz, cognitive-load)
- Its change carries its sign in its text, +4% or -3%, since the arrow is hidden from a screen reader and its colour is not a meaning. (von-restorff-effect)
- The stats of one row count the same kind of thing over the same period, so they are read as one set. (law-of-similarity, law-of-proximity)
- They are the few figures the screen exists for and come first on it; every other number is a row of a table or a description list. (pareto-principle, serial-position-effect)
- Every figure is counted by the app from its records and never typed in, since a number nobody measured is a false claim. (cognitive-bias)
