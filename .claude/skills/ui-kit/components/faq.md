# faq

The questions people phone to ask, parking, allergies, gift cards, the delivery area, each a line that opens on its answer with no script.

## Placement

- carte.offer — fits: the list the page is for, at the reading width, when the page is the questions themselves
- carte.main — fits: under the offer, the questions that come after reading it
- marketing.main — fits: across the reading width, near the end of the page, after what it answers
- split.main — fits: under the opening's two halves
- poster.main — fits: a short list, below the fold
- board.main — fits: a tile of its own, the questions one under another
- journal.main — fits: the practical part of the narrow column, which a list of questions reads well in
- stacked.main — fits: one card of the grid, a few short questions
- focus.main — fits: beside the one task it answers questions about, in the 36rem column
- console.main — fits: the admin's view of what a visitor reads, in its column of data
- workspace.detail — fits: one block of the chosen item
- * — never: a side column stays short, an opening says what the page is, a bar is navigation and a band asks for one thing

## Limits

- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)
- entries — 12; advice 7 (choice-overload, millers-law)
- per-page — 1 (choice-overload)

## Behaviour

- It sits on one page, the visit or the questions page, and every other page that raises a question links there rather than repeating the list. (choice-overload, hicks-law)
- A question is the visitor's own words and a single line, so the list is scanned before anything is opened. (mental-model, chunking)
- A bare tag draws the questions the owner edits in the admin, and questions written on the tag are the brief's own: never an invented policy, price or opening day. (teslers-law, cognitive-bias)
- The most asked question comes first and the one that settles a visit, parking or booking, is never last in a long list. (serial-position-effect, pareto-principle)
- An answer says what to do, a phone number or an address in full, so the visitor leaves the list with the answer and not another question. (goal-gradient-effect, zeigarnik-effect)
- Linked from the navigation as #questions, it carries id="questions" on its own tag, or the link lands nowhere. (flow, peak-end-rule)
