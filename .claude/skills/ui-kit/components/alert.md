# alert

A message in a panel with its icon, a title and the actions that answer it: what just happened, or what needs doing before going on.

## Placement

- *.main — fits: the answer to what was just done, a form sent or an action taken, at the top of main or in the form's place
- workspace.detail — fits: above the chosen item, what just happened to it
- * — never: an opening, a bar, a title strip, a side column, the offer, the band and the footer hold only what their region names, and a closure is the site-header's status line on every page

## Limits

- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)
- actions — 2 (hicks-law)
- per-page — 2; advice 1 (selective-attention, von-restorff-effect)

## Behaviour

- It is read out the moment it renders, as role alert, so it appears only as the answer to something just done or a state that needs acting on, never as standing content. (flow, selective-attention)
- On a public page it is only a form's answer: the success in the form's place, an error above its fields saying what would be accepted. (flow, postels-law)
- A closure or a change of hours is the site-header's status line, which every page shows, and never an alert. (selective-attention, working-memory)
- Info and success are drawn alike and only their icon differs, so its title says which in a word, such as Sent or Saved. (von-restorff-effect)
- Its actions are at most two, the way to fix or to undo what it reports, and neither is the page's primary. (hicks-law, von-restorff-effect)
