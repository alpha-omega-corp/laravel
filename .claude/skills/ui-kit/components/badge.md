# badge

A short label on the thing it qualifies: a status, a count or a kind, in one of four tones.

## Placement

- *.main — fits: beside the one thing it qualifies, inside a section, a tile, a row or a panel
- workspace.list — fits: in a row, as its status or its count
- workspace.detail — fits: beside one fact of the chosen item
- * — never: in an opening a badge reads as an advert, a title strip gives a status in its page-heading's meta, and the bar, a side column, the offer, the band, the gallery and the footer hold only what their region names

## Behaviour

- It qualifies the one thing beside it in a word or two, and an item carries one at most. (selective-attention, cognitive-load)
- One status wears one tone on every page, and its word says what the tone means, since a colour alone does not. (law-of-similarity, von-restorff-effect)
- It never stands alone as a banner or a promotion, a New or a discount over an opening, since visitors skip what looks like an advert. (selective-attention)
- It is a label and never a control: something to press is a button or a link. (jakobs-law, law-of-similarity)
- On a site it states only what the brief does, a real status or a real kind, never a claim such as Best seller that nobody measured. (cognitive-bias)
