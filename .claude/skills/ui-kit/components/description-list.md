# description-list

The facts of one record as terms and their values, in a panel with an optional title: what an admin screen, an item's detail or a review before confirming shows of it.

## Placement

- console.main — fits: one panel across the column, each term a third of its width from sm
- stacked.main — fits: one card of the grid, a third of the width from lg, so each term is a word or two
- focus.main — fits: the review before the task's last step, above the action that confirms it
- workspace.detail — fits: the chosen item's facts, under its page-heading
- * — never: a site's facts are its prefabs, the schedule for the hours and the map for the way in, or a section's words, which a direction draws

## Limits

- items — 9; advice 7 (millers-law, chunking)
- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)

## Behaviour

- Each row is one fact, a term of a word or two and the value as the record holds it, written as the owner wrote it. (law-of-proximity, postels-law)
- Past seven rows it becomes two lists, each titled with what its rows are about. (chunking, millers-law)
- In focus it is the review before the last step, each term in the words of the field that asked for it and the confirming action right under it. (goal-gradient-effect, working-memory)
- Across a wide column it is `striped`, since there the tint keeps each value on the line of its term across the gap between them. (law-of-common-region)
- It shows and never edits: a value is changed in a form reached from the heading's action. (mental-model)
