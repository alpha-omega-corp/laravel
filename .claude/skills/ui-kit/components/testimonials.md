# testimonials

What customers said, in their words and under their name: a quotation, who said it and what they are to the business, never unsigned and never written for them.

## Placement

- marketing.main — fits: across the reading width, two or three across and one on a phone
- split.main — fits: under the opening's two halves
- carte.main — fits: under the offer, from those who have ordered it
- poster.main — fits: one or two, below the fold
- board.main — fits: a tile of its own, one quotation across in a third of the page
- journal.story — fits: one voice inside the story, in the narrow column
- journal.main — fits: the voices in the narrow column, one across
- stacked.main — fits: one card of the grid, a third of the width
- focus.main — fits: the one voice that settles the task, in the 36rem column
- * — never: a quotation needs a line length a side column does not have, an opening is the business speaking for itself, and a band asks for one thing

## Limits

- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)
- testimonials — 9; advice 3 (choice-overload, working-memory)
- per-page — 1 (law-of-similarity)

## Behaviour

- A session building a page never writes one: a bare tag draws what the owner entered in the admin, and with nothing entered it draws nothing, since an invented customer is a false statement under the firm's name. (cognitive-bias, teslers-law)
- Every quotation is signed with a name and what that person is to the business, and one with no name is left out rather than signed “un client”. (cognitive-bias, mental-model)
- Three read whole are worth nine skimmed: the strongest first, the most specific last, and the rest left in the admin. (serial-position-effect, peak-end-rule)
- A quotation is kept to what was said, two or three sentences, and is never shortened into praise its author did not give. (cognitive-load, postels-law)
- A portrait is the person's own, and with none the name stands alone: never a stock face beside a real name. (cognitive-bias, law-of-similarity)
- It stands after the proof it speaks about, the work or the offer, and before the page's ask. (goal-gradient-effect, serial-position-effect)
- Its arrangement is chosen on its tag and the direction's look stays: `variant="lead"` sets the first quotation large across the column with the others under it, for one voice that carries the page; `variant="wall"` flows them down columns at their own heights, for many short ones; bare, it is the direction's own. (von-restorff-effect, aesthetic-usability-effect)
