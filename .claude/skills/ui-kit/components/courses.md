# courses

What can be signed up for and whether there is still room: a row per session led by its date, then its name, who it is for, what it costs, the places left and the way to enrol.

## Placement

- carte.offer — fits: the list the page is for, in the widest column, the date beside each name
- carte.main — fits: under the offer when the offer holds the price list
- marketing.main — fits: across the reading width, the date beside the name and above it in a narrow column
- split.main — fits: under the opening's two halves
- poster.main — fits: the next few, below the fold, since a long list is carte's
- board.main — fits: a tile of its own, the date above the name in a third of the page
- journal.main — fits: the practical part of the narrow column, the date above the name
- stacked.main — fits: one card of the grid, a third of the width
- focus.main — fits: the sessions the one task chooses among, in the 36rem column
- console.main — fits: the admin's view of what a visitor reads, in its column of data
- workspace.detail — fits: one block of the chosen item
- * — never: nothing wide goes in a side column, an opening says what the page is and a band asks for one thing

## Limits

- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)
- courses — 12; advice 8 (choice-overload, hicks-law)
- per-page — 1 (occams-razor)

## Behaviour

- It lives on the one page whose role is the programme, and home shows the next two or three and links to the rest rather than the season twice. (choice-overload, pareto-principle)
- A bare tag draws the sessions the owner keeps in the admin, those already over left off, and one written on the tag is the brief's own: never an invented session with a real date, a price or a number of places. (cognitive-bias, teslers-law)
- Every row leads with its date as the business says it, du 9 au 14 février, the machine's date kept on the time element, so the list reads in the order a visitor plans in. (serial-position-effect, postels-law)
- The places left are the owner's figure or absent, and a full session keeps its row and loses its button, so a visitor sees it existed and looks at the next date. (mental-model, zeigarnik-effect)
- The few places left are the one highlight of the row and are never said of a session that is not nearly full: scarcity nobody counted is pressure, not information. (von-restorff-effect, cognitive-bias)
- It is not the agenda: an event is something to come to, a course something to enrol in, so each row ends in the way to do it and a page that has both gives them different pages. (mental-model, law-of-similarity)
- Linked from the navigation as #cours, it carries id="cours" on its own tag, or the link lands nowhere. (flow, peak-end-rule)
- Its arrangement is chosen on its tag and the direction's look stays: `variant="cards"` makes each session a card, for a few that each deserve a look; bare, it is the direction's own. (law-of-common-region, aesthetic-usability-effect)
