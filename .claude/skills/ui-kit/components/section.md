# section

A titled band of the site's own words: an eyebrow, a heading with its mark, a lead, and whatever its slot holds, which is where a story or a paragraph about the house is written.

## Placement

- carte.lead — first: at level 1, the page's one h1, its name and one line with nothing to scroll past before the list
- board.hero — first: at level 1 in place of the hero, a short opening above the board
- marketing.main — fits: at level 2 in the reading width, far enough from its neighbours to stand on its own
- split.main — fits: at level 2, under the opening's two halves
- carte.main — fits: at level 2, under the list and its hours
- poster.main — fits: at level 2, below the fold and plain, the cover being the page's loud part
- board.main — fits: one tile, its head and words a third of the page wide from lg
- journal.story — fits: the story's opening in the one max-w-3xl column, words and nothing wide
- journal.main — fits: the practical words, how to book and how to find the door, in the same narrow column
- *.visit — fits: only as the line of address under the hours where there is no map, a lead and nothing else
- poster.facts — fits: only as the line of address beside the hours where there is no map, a lead and nothing else
- carte.offer — never: the offer is the list itself, and its heading is the lead
- * — never: an opening is the hero's, the gallery is pictures, a band is the page's ask and a foot the footer's, and an app screen titles itself with page-heading

## Limits

- title — 48 characters (law-of-pragnanz)
- lead — 160 characters (cognitive-load)

## Behaviour

- Its level is 1 only where it opens the page in place of a hero, in carte's lead or board's opening, and 2 everywhere else, so every page has exactly one h1. (chunking, mental-model)
- Anywhere but a side column it carries a title, so every block of the page is one a visitor can scan to and the navigation can name. (chunking)
- Two sections never follow one another in a region: the second joins the first, or something of another shape, a photograph or a list, goes between them. (law-of-similarity)
- Its words are the site's own, from the brief, with no “why choose us” and no history or promise nobody gave, and a section with nothing to say is left out rather than filled. (occams-razor, cognitive-bias)
- Linked from the navigation as #la-maison, it carries id="la-maison" on its own tag, or the link lands nowhere. (flow, peak-end-rule)
