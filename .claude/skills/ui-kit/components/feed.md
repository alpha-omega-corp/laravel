# feed

A timeline of what happened, one entry a line with its time and the rule running behind the markers: a record's history, or the latest activity on a dashboard.

## Placement

- console.main — fits: under its section-heading, the history of what the screen manages
- stacked.main — fits: one card of the grid, the latest entries
- workspace.detail — fits: the chosen item's history, under its tabs
- * — never: it is an app's activity log, while a site's timeline is a section's words in journal's column, which a direction draws

## Limits

- items — 9; advice 7 (millers-law, working-memory)

## Behaviour

- Each entry is one sentence saying who did what, and every time in it is written one way, all relative or all dates. (law-of-similarity, cognitive-load)
- It runs in one order, the newest first on a dashboard card and the oldest first in a record's history, and one screen never mixes the two. (jakobs-law, mental-model)
- A tone marks the one or two entries that differ, a step completed or a problem, and the entry's words say so too, since a marker's colour alone means nothing. (von-restorff-effect)
- Past nine entries the rest of the history is a screen of its own, linked from the section's heading. (working-memory)
- With no entries it renders nothing, so its section shows an empty-state rather than a heading over nothing. (flow)
