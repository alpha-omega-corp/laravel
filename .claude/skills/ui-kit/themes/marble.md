# marble

White marble, near-black ink and a black pill for the action, blue kept for links and violet for badges, under a heavy geometric face set tight. A hard-working tool's brochure.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='marble']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

The colours are few and each has one job. The ink is a near-black (`#202020`)
rather than black, and it is what the spec direction fills its action with. The
accent is a blue darkened to 4.9:1 on white so that it can be a link's colour;
it is never the action's fill there. The highlight is a violet, for badges, a
flag and a count. The second ground is a cool grey band that breaks a long run
of white. In the dark it turns to an onyx page with the same blue lifted and the
violet softened. The display face is Plus Jakarta Sans at 800, tracked in by
0.04em at its largest; the body is Inter. Controls are full pills
(`--radius-control: 9999px`) and panels a 12px corner, with no shadow anywhere:
a panel is a hairline.

Productive, exact, confident: a product sheet blown up to exhibition size.

## Costs

All white and one near-black: it needs content dense enough to carry it, a
product's screens, numbers, names. On a page of three paragraphs and a
photograph it reads as empty, and on a warm trade (a bakery, an inn) it reads as
software. The blue is a link colour and nothing more, so a site that wants a
brand colour on its buttons wants another palette.
