# clay

Terracotta and olive on warm paper, a serif cut with a chisel, tiles with a short earthen shadow. Hand-made, warm, in season.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='clay']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

**The olive is for what is in season, not for a button.** The terracotta is the link and the action in both schemes; the olive marks a badge, a date, what is new this month. Panels carry a short shadow in the terracotta itself, so a card reads as a tile laid on the page rather than floating over it.

## Costs

An earth palette on warm paper turns muddy at density: in `console` and `workspace` a screen of rows loses its hierarchy, since every surface is some shade of clay. Its serif is cut for headings and tires in long tables.
