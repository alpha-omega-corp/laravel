# quill

Bone paper and a blue-black ink, the navy of a bound volume and a little brass, in a reading serif with hairlines and small corners. Measured, credentialed, unhurried.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='quill']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

**The brass is for what was earned, not for what is sold.** The highlight marks
an admission, a year, a certification; the navy is the link and the button.
The body leading is the loosest of the set, 1.7, because these pages are read
rather than scanned, and the reading width is the narrowest, 70rem.

## Costs

Quiet to the point of stillness: nothing in it says *today* or *now*, so a shop
or a café drawn in it reads as closed. In `board` the hairlines give a tile no
weight, and in `poster` a serif at one size and a navy button are not a cover.
