# plum

Aubergine on a blush-white page with a little antique gold, a high-contrast serif over a geometric sans, pill buttons in small capitals. Elegant, quiet, a little theatrical.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='plum']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

**The serif is large or it is not there.** Its hairlines vanish below a title's size, so it is set at `--text-title` and up and never in a caption; the sans under it does the reading. Controls are pills and panels are almost square: the contrast between the two is the palette's signature, and rounding the panels to match loses it.

## Costs

Uppercase tracked buttons and a display serif are an evening dress: on a builder's or a garage's page they read as a costume. Dense screens lose the long shadow that separates its panels, and the pills crowd a toolbar.
