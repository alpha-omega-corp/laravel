# forno

Ash paper and a soot ink by day, a charcoal page and a live ember by night, a flame for the highlight, in a heavy grotesque cut like a painted shop sign with tight corners and a warm shadow straight down. The wood oven after dark.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='forno']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

Locanda's sister for the night. In the light the accent is a brick ember
(`#a62f14`) with white on it, 6.9:1; in the dark it is a live coal (`#ef6a3a`)
with the soot on it, 6.1:1, because a glowing orange is the one colour that
reads at eleven at night and white on it would not. The highlight is the flame
(`#f2a93b`), with the soot on it. Bricolage Grotesque at 800 over Work Sans; a
6px corner on panels and controls, a hairline round every panel and a short
warm shadow under it, as from a lamp hung over the counter. The secondary
button is outlined in the ink rather than lifted.

## Mood

Loud, late, hot: a sign over a door that is still open.

## Costs

The display face is heavy and the corners tight: on a long page of prose it
shouts. It wants short lines, big prices and a photograph of fire; a calm
dinner room is hearth's ember or vellum, not this.
