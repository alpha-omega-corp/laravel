# sandstone

Warm editorial paper with a burnt-sienna accent and a serif display face. Considered,
printed, unhurried.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='sandstone']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

## Mood

Considered, printed, unhurried.

## Pairs with

- focus — best
- marketing — best
- stacked — good
- console — fair
- workspace — fair

## Costs

Generous with space. In `console` and `workspace`, where the point is rows per screen,
it costs about a third of the density. Use it there deliberately or not at all.
