# marigold

Sunny yellow on cream, black keycap buttons with yellow labels, and a thick ink edge on every
panel with a hard shadow straight down. Loud, cheerful, hands-on.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='marigold']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

**The yellow is not the light scheme's accent.** Yellow on cream is 1.6:1, too
faint for a link or an eyebrow, so in the light the accent is the ink and the
yellow is what sits on it: the label on a black button, the `canvas-alt` bands,
the highlight. In the dark the yellow reads at 12:1 and takes the accent back.
`text-accent` is therefore black on a light page — which is right for a link,
and the reason not to lean on the accent alone to say “this is the brand”.

## Costs

Spends everything on edge and shadow: in `console` and `workspace` every row and panel gets
a 2px outline, and a dense screen of keycaps is tiring. Keep it to pages that are mostly
openings, offers and calls to act.
