# fresh

Green and orange on a tinted page, four-step elevation, and panels that lift off it.
Bright, energetic, current.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='fresh']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

## Mood

Bright, energetic, current.

## Pairs with

- marketing — best
- stacked — best
- focus — good
- console — fair
- workspace — fair

## Costs

Spends its budget on elevation and on movement. In `console` and `workspace` the screen
is mostly one dense surface, so the theme pays for depth it never uses.
