# alpine

Snow and glacier grounds, a lake blue that reads from far off and a rescue orange for what is urgent, round and quick with long cold shadows. Clear, outdoors, in season.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='alpine']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

**The orange means urgent, so spend it on urgency.** A closed route, the last
two places on a date, today's conditions: the highlight is a rescue jacket, and
a page that puts it on every badge has cried wolf by the second screen. Panels
have no edge in the light and float on a shadow; in the dark the shadow shows
nothing, so the edge comes back.

## Costs

Built for a photograph behind it: on a page of text and tables the pale grounds
and the floating panels look unfinished. In `console` and `workspace` the long
shadows stack row on row and blur what a dense screen has to keep apart.
