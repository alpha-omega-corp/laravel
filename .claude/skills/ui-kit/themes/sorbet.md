# sorbet

Peach paper, a raspberry that reads as a link and a cool blue beside it, in a rounded face, with stickers for panels. Playful, clear, for a Saturday.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='sorbet']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

**Playful in its shapes, serious in its contrast.** The raspberry is 5.9:1 on the peach page and the blue 5.6:1, so the fun is in the corners and the face, never in pale text. Buttons press down two pixels onto a hard drop, and panels have no edge in the light: on `canvas-alt` they are stickers on coloured paper, which is where they look best.

## Costs

A rounded face and pill buttons make everything friendly, including what should not be: a refusal, a price, a legal notice. It is wrong for any business chosen on standing, and its soft panels blur a dense screen.
