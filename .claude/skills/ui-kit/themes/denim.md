# denim

Indigo on a washed page with the rust of a rivet, a wide grotesque set tight, panels edged with two lines of stitching. Sturdy, plain-spoken, made to be used.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='denim']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

**Two lines of stitching, drawn as a shadow.** A panel's edge is the rule, a gap of the panel's own surface, and the rule again; raised, the outer line turns to the accent. It costs four pixels round every panel, which a grid has to leave between its cells. The rust is the highlight only: one badge, one figure, never a second button colour.

## Costs

The stitched edge needs air: in a tight grid the double lines of neighbouring panels read as one thick rule. Its wide display face runs out of line on a phone, and in `journal` a narrow column sets it two words a line.
