# basilico

Flour, tomato and basil: a cream page and a basil-black ink, a tomato red for the action, a pale basil leaf for the highlight, a friendly slab-cut serif over a round sans, pill controls and soft round panels. The trattoria on the corner.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='basilico']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

The three colours of a Margherita, kept apart so each has one job. The tomato
(`#b8241c`) is the action, with white on it, 6.3:1; in the dark, over a garden
green that is nearly black, it ripens to `#e5554a` with the ink's near-black on
it, 5.4:1. The basil (`#b9d89a`) is the highlight, a flag or the day's offer,
with the ink on it; it is never text. Young Serif at its one weight over
Figtree; panels a 20px corner that lift a little off the flour, controls full
pills, and the secondary button an ink outline.

## Costs

Three named colours make it read as Italian at once, which is the point and
the limit: on anything that is not Italian food it is a flag. The serif has one
weight, so a page that needs a bold heading and a light one gets size alone.
