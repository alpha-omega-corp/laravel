# nocturne

Violet and magenta on a near-white page that turns into a night sky in the dark, where panels
are edged by a faint glow rather than a shadow. Modern, electric, after hours.

## Tokens

`[data-palette='nocturne']` in `resources/css/themes.css`, which is the one
place they are written. Every colour is a `light-dark()` pair, so this palette
is declared once and answers both schemes — which half applies is the *other*
axis and is not this file's business. See [README.md](README.md).

## Costs

Its glow is its depth: in the light scheme it reads as a tinted shadow and is quieter than the
dark one, so a site that wants the night should pin the dark. Violet says “tech” or “club”
before it says “bakery”.
