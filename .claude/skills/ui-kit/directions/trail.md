# trail

The outdoors in season: one landscape edge to edge that dissolves into the page at its foot, the name set over that horizon, the dates and the ways on painted on signpost boards, the facts in a strip, and a ridge line where the page meets the ground of its footer.

## Layouts

- poster — one landscape and the season’s dates
- journal — a route told day by day
- marketing — the long page
- split — the outing beside its dates and meeting point

## Scheme

`any`

## Palettes

- alpine — best
- lagoon — good
- orchard — good
- sandstone — good
- fresh — fair
- clay — good

## Suits

a mountain guide, a ski school, a campsite, an outdoor venue

## Looks

The frames: the nav is left as the kit draws it; the hero frame, where the site chose no arrangement, loses its top padding, its wrap's width cap and its side padding, so the photograph runs square-cornered to both edges of the window, with `clamp(1rem, 3vw, 2.5rem)` under it; the main keeps the kit's frame, its sections `clamp(4rem, 8vw, 6.5rem)` apart; the band is the deep accent with the on-accent colour as its text, and on it the accent becomes that ink, so the eyebrow and the primary button invert and the secondary is transparent inside a 1px edge of the ink; the footer is the second ground with no top rule, and a 2.75rem ridge of peaks cut from that ground rises above it — over the page after a 2.75rem margin, or into the band when the band ends the main, which keeps 2.75rem more padding at its foot for it. Every button on the page is a pill.
