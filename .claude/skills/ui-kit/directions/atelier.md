# atelier

Work on white walls: one plate hung inside the page's margins with its label under it, the work in strict grids of numbered plates, everything that is only words an index hanging from hairlines, and the page closed by a title block ruled in the ink — no card, no tint and no accent in the chrome, so the photographs are the only loud thing.

## Layouts

- board — the work as plates on one wall
- journal — one project told at length, plates between the paragraphs
- poster — one picture and the name
- marketing — the long page

## Scheme

`any`

## Palettes

- vellum — best
- harbour — good
- quill — good
- sandstone — good
- concrete — fair
- clay — fair
- plum — fair

## Suits

an architect, a photographer, a designer, a maker whose work is looked at

## Looks

The frames: the nav on the canvas edge to edge, with no rule under it; the hero inside the page's margins, never bled, its top padding cut to 0.75–1.5rem so the plate hangs close under the nav; the main with 5–9rem between its sections; the band on the canvas itself with the ink as its text and 4–7rem of padding, since its frame is drawn by the component; the footer with no rule of its own, only its type made small. Across the page the eyebrows are 13px labels at regular weight in the soft ink, neither uppercase nor tracked, a section heading is 22–28px, no photograph has a rounded corner and nothing stands on a card: a head hangs from a 1px hairline across the full width of its column, which is what parts one section, or one tile of a board, from the next. `--hero-min-height` (60svh, 58svh on a phone) is the most the opening's plate may stand, not the least.

- site-header — the bar one line at 12px, the phone in the soft ink at regular weight; the pages at 13px, their underline a 1px hairline in the text's own colour; the action a line of type in the ink over a hairline, with no box, no ground and no padding at its sides, the hairline 2px on hover.
- hero — laid out only when the site chose no arrangement: one column, the plate first, 16/9 across the opening and at most `--hero-min-height` tall (4/5 on a phone), the label under it as a grid of two columns while the opening holds two of 20rem — the eyebrow across both, the name down the left, the line, the actions and the note together at the top of the right. In every hero the name is in the display face at 36–56px, the line 16px, the note 13px; the actions are lines of type over a hairline, 2rem apart, the first in the ink and the second in the soft ink over a hairline of the rule colour; the facts stand across the label 1rem under it, as many 9rem columns as fit, each figure at 24–32px set over a 13px line saying what it counts.
- features — one plate a row, 2.5–5rem between rows: the photograph 3/2, at most 72svh or 36rem tall, taking three parts of the row, and its label the fourth, beside it at the plate's foot as a label stands by a picture and under it where the column holds only one; the title 20px, the body 14px, the icon in the soft ink, the link 13px in the ink over a hairline.
- section — the head under its hairline across the whole column, the title at 22–28px, the lead at 16px and 34rem at most.
- media — square corners and no scrim; 16/9 in the opening, 3/2 in a feature.
- marks — hidden: no title on the page carries one.
- projects — plates: three across at most and as many as the column holds at 14rem, 2.5rem between columns and 2.5–4rem between rows, no card, edge, ground or shadow, the photograph square-cornered and an empty one a sheet inside a hairline; the label under it indented 2.75rem, a 12px number in the soft ink in that margin counted from 01, the name first at 18px, then the place and year, the kind and the summary as 13px lines, the summary 36 characters wide at most; the figure a 13px line of the label with its value semibold in the body face, not a number set large and not ruled off. Laid out as rows or an index, each item hangs from a hairline.
- gallery — the work uncropped: four across at most and as many as the column holds at 13rem, every picture whole in a 4/3 cell with no ground behind it, standing at the cell's foot on its left edge so a row shares one line; the caption a 13px label with its number in the margin, counted from 01.
- listings — plates as the projects, in the same grid with the same numbered label; the place a 13px line, the facts 0.5rem under it, the price 14px semibold in the body face; the status a square tab in the canvas colour with ink text, cut out of the plate's top corner.
- catalogue — plates, three across at most at 14rem, rows at their own height, no card: the photograph square-cornered, the name in the display face at 18px, the price beside it at 14px, the description at 13px.
- team — the person last and small: as many 10rem columns as fit, 2.5rem apart, the portraits in greyscale with square corners, no card, the name in the display face at 17px, the role and the line at 13px in the soft ink.
- services — the index: one column of rows, each under a hairline and the list closed by one, a 12px number in the soft ink in a 2.75rem margin counted from 01, the name at 18px in a column of its own (14rem or 30% of the row) so every line starts on one edge, the line beside it and the price at 13px, regular weight, at the row's end; no icon, no card. Arranged another way the items keep their hairline and the icon is in the soft ink.
- process — the steps across, 2.5rem apart, each under a 1px rule: the number a 12px label in the soft ink in the body face with 1.5rem under it, then the name at 18px, the line, and how long it takes at 13px in the soft ink.
- testimonials — the text on a wall: no card, each under a hairline, the quotation in the display face at 20–24px, who said it at 13px with the name at medium weight, the portrait square-cornered and in greyscale.
- credentials — the smallest label on the page: the name at 14px, the detail at 13px, the logos in greyscale.
- pricing — the plans as columns 2.5rem apart, each under a 1px rule in the ink and the one put forward — the one with the flag or the primary button — under a rule of 3px; no card, no ring, the price at 32px, the ticks in the soft ink, the note at 13px.
- jobs — rows of the index: the name in the display face at 18px, the line at 13px, the place and the hours as 13px words in the soft ink on one line with a point between them, not chips, the arrow in the soft ink at the row's end.
- courses — rows of the index: the date a 13px label in the body face with tabular figures, the name in the display face at 18px, the time and the facts at 13px, the price at 14px.
- events — as the courses: the date a 13px label, the name in the display face at 18px, the time and the description at 13px, the picture square-cornered.
- menu — the price list: one column of 46rem at the wall's left edge, each section's title a 13px label in the soft ink at regular weight over a rule in the ink, the name in the display face at 18px, the price at 14px at the row's end with no leader, the description at 13px, a hairline between items.
- faq — one column of 46rem: the question in the display face at 18px, its own marker gone and a plus in the soft ink at the row's end that turns to a minus when it is open.
- schedule — no panel, 28rem at most: the title a 13px label in the soft ink over a hairline in the ink, the days straight under it, the day names at regular weight, the note at 13px.
- booking — no panel, under one hairline in the ink: two columns while the column holds two of 18rem, the title and the words down the left and the form, or what it says once sent, down the right; the words at 16px and 34rem at most.
- quote — as the booking, the words on a third and the form on two, wrapping to one column where it is narrow; the note at 13px.
- donate — no panel, under one hairline in the ink; the words at 16px and 34rem at most, the note at 13px.
- map — no panel: no edge, corner, ground or shadow, the map in greyscale, and the address and the way there a 13px line under it with no rule between, the way there in the ink over a hairline.
- cta-band — a title block, as at the foot of a drawing: one frame of 1px in the ink, the invitation in its first cell with the title at 28–44px and the line at 16px, the actions as lines of type stacked at the foot of the next, and a cell for every detail, 11rem at least, a 12px label over a 14px value; every cell ruled off from its neighbours in the ink, and the cells wrap where the column is narrow so the last row runs to the frame.
- site-footer — a colophon: the blurb, the pages and the contact at 13px, their heads 12px labels in the soft ink.
