# market

A market stall in the sun: a wash of the palette behind the page, the stall's panels as a board
of glass cards — what is in season, when it is open, the way in — and a warm band before the
footer.

## Layouts

- board — the board of glass panels its CSS already lays main out as
- split — the stall on one side, what is in season and when on the other
- marketing — the long page

## Scheme

`any`

## Looks

The frames: the page under `--page-wash`; the nav sticky at the top; the hero padded at the foot
only; the main frame a board — from `lg` a twelve-column grid `--section-gap` apart, the
features, the menu, the catalogue and any section across all twelve, the hours on five beside
the map on seven when the two are next to each other — either alone takes all twelve — one
column below `lg`; the band tinted with the highlight; the footer frame
plain with the footer itself on glass.

- glass — the board's sections, the header and the footer are glass: the raised surface at 72% over the wash, blurred (`--panel-backdrop`), a soft rule edge, the panel's corner, 1.5rem in; the map unpadded; the schedule's own panel takes the same values.
- eyebrows — pills on the highlight at 22% with a highlight dot, and today's status in the header the same.
- marks — a 0.625rem dot of the highlight.
- hero — two panels side by side while the hero's own column holds both, sharing their edges, stacked in split's column, the copy on glass with 2rem in, the photograph 4/3 on `--radius-media`, a highlight dot before the note.
- features — a bento board: from `lg` a twelve-column grid 1.25rem apart, the first item seven columns and two rows, the others five; each item glass, one column, its photograph on top — 4/3 for the first, 16/9 for the rest.
- menu — each section a glass card, as many to a row as fit at 22rem, the leaders kept.
- schedule — glass with hairlines, closed days in ink-soft.
- catalogue — rows: as many columns as fit at 20rem, each item a horizontal glass row with a square photograph or frame on the left and the price at the right, semibold.
- map — glass, unpadded.
- cta-band — the copy on the left, the actions on the right.
- site-footer — glass, three columns.
- projects — the catalogue's card stood up: each project glass with 1rem in, its photograph or frame inset on `--radius-media`, the place and the year an eyebrow's pill with its highlight dot, the figure over a hairline as soft as the glass's edge; the cards 1.25rem apart.
- services — the catalogue's rows: as many columns as fit at 20rem, each service a glass row with its icon on a square of the highlight's tint at the left, the name, the price at the right, semibold, and the line under them; an arrangement keeps the glass, the square and the weight and lays the row out its own way.
- process — a glass card a step in place of the ink rule, 1.25rem in, its number in the ink on a round of the highlight's tint, a highlight dot before the duration as before the hero's note; a timeline and a row keep their own rule.
- testimonials — glass like a team member: the quotation opens on the mark's dot, and who said it stands under a soft hairline.
- pricing — each plan a glass card with a soft hairline over what it includes; the flag a pill of the highlight; the plan drawn stronger, found by its flag or its primary button, takes the accent as a 2px edge since the glass drops the markup's ring.
- listings — the projects' card: glass with 1rem in, the photograph or frame inset on `--radius-media` with the status a pill of the highlight over its corner, the facts over a soft hairline, and the price the catalogue's — the body's face, semibold, at the right.
- credentials — small glass tiles with 1rem in, in place of the rule over each; a row of logos and a ruled list keep their own.
- jobs — the list one glass panel with 1.5rem in, as the agenda's, soft hairlines between the positions, the place and the hours pills on the highlight's tint, the arrow in the ink on a round of the same tint; as cards, each position is the palette's panel.
- quote — glass, as the section's panel, a highlight dot before the note under the button.
- donate — glass; the account stands on a block of the band's tint of the highlight on `--radius-media`, its labels in the ink, and a highlight dot comes before the note; split rules the account off at the side instead.
- courses — the agenda's panel: the list one glass panel with 1.5rem in and soft hairlines between the sessions, the price semibold, the places left a pill of the highlight and a full session's the same pill on the quiet ground.
