# market

A market stall in the sun: a wash of the palette behind the page, the stall's panels as a board
of glass cards — what is in season, when it is open, the way in — and a warm band before the
footer.

## Layouts

- board — the board of glass panels its CSS already lays main out as
- split — the stall on one side, what is in season and when on the other
- marketing — the long page

## Scheme

`any`

## Palettes

- fresh — best
- orchard — best
- sandstone — good
- blossom — fair

## Suits

a farm shop, a market stall, a box scheme

## Looks

The frames: the page under `--page-wash`; the nav sticky at the top; the hero padded at the foot
only; the main frame a board — from `lg` a twelve-column grid `--section-gap` apart, the
features, the menu, the catalogue and any section across all twelve, the hours on five beside
the map on seven, one column below `lg`; the band tinted with the highlight; the footer frame
plain with the footer itself on glass.

- glass — the board's sections, the header and the footer are glass: the raised surface at 72% over the wash, blurred (`--panel-backdrop`), a soft rule edge, the panel's corner, 1.5rem in; the map unpadded; the schedule's own panel takes the same values.
- eyebrows — pills on the highlight at 22% with a highlight dot, and today's status in the header the same.
- marks — a 0.625rem dot of the highlight.
- hero — the split `1fr 1fr` from `lg`, the two panels sharing their edges, the copy on glass with 2rem in, the photograph 4/3 on `--radius-media`, a highlight dot before the note.
- features — a bento board: from `lg` a twelve-column grid 1.25rem apart, the first item seven columns and two rows, the others five; each item glass, one column, its photograph on top — 4/3 for the first, 16/9 for the rest.
- menu — each section a glass card, two to a row from `lg`, the leaders kept.
- schedule — glass with hairlines, closed days in ink-soft.
- catalogue — rows: two columns from `md`, each item a horizontal glass row with a square photograph or frame on the left and the price at the right, semibold.
- map — glass, unpadded.
- cta-band — the copy on the left, the actions on the right.
- site-footer — glass, three columns.
