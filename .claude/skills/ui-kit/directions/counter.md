# counter

A bright counter that gets to the point: the name beside a portrait photograph with the facts
under it, the week's hours as a strip across the page, the carte in rows, and a dark band
asking for the order. Warm paper, framed, practical.

## Layouts

- split — the name and the facts first, as a counter would put them
- carte — the carte in rows from the first screen, the hours pinned beside it
- board — the produce, the hours and the way in on one board
- marketing — the long page

## Scheme

`any`

## Palettes

- sandstone — best
- orchard — best
- harbour — good
- fresh — good
- blossom — fair
- vellum — fair

## Suits

a butcher, a bakery, a café, a shop with a counter, lunch as much as dinner, a trade or a practice that keeps it plain

## Looks

The frames: the nav with no top padding; the hero on the page's second ground; the band inverse
— the ink as its ground, the canvas as its text, the primary button still the accent and the
secondary transparent with a canvas edge; the footer on the second ground.

- site-header — the bar a full-bleed dark strip with canvas text, today's status after an accent dot, the phone bold; the brand bold in the display face at `text-xl`.
- hero — the name beside the photograph while the hero's own column holds both, the photograph a 3/4 portrait in the hero band from `lg` and 4/3 anywhere else — in split's column the hero stacks, and a portrait the column's width would be taller than the screen; the facts under a 2px ink rule, three columns, their values at `text-figure`.
- features — blocks that alternate: from `md` every second item puts its photograph on the right.
- marks — a 2.5rem by 2px bar of the ink rather than the accent.
- menu — the carte in rows: a grid of sections, as many 20rem columns as fit, the title across all of them; each section title sans, small, uppercase and tracked over a 2px ink rule; no leader; each row the name at medium weight and the price semibold at the end, a hairline between items.
- schedule — the hours standing out: no panel, a full-bleed strip of the second ground, joined to the hero when it opens the main frame; from `lg` the week in seven columns, each day's name small, uppercase and tracked above its hours in the display face at `text-lg`, with no lines between; two columns below `lg`.
- catalogue — a rail: 16rem cards in a row that scrolls and snaps (80% of the width on a phone), square photographs and frames, the price in the accent, semibold.
- map — the panel edged in 1.5px of ink, the caption on the second ground.
- cta-band — the title and the text on the ink ground.
- site-footer — three columns, small text.
