# spec

A working tool's brochure, drawn like its own interface on white: the promise set huge, bold and tight beside a screen of the product, every button, tag, badge and link in the bar a full pill, the proof in grey and the numbers large, panels parted by hairlines rather than shadows, and a single rainbow edge turning round the action of the opening and of the dark panel that closes the page. Tight inside a section, spacious between them.

## Layouts

- split — the promise beside the product's screen, the direction's own opening
- marketing — the long page: promise and screen, proof, plans, the dark close
- board — the features and the plans as tiles

## Scheme

`any`

## Palettes

- marble — best
- graphite — good
- harbour — fair

## Suits

software, an app, a platform, a coworking space or an agency that sells a working tool or a measurable result

## Looks

The frames: the nav and the footer keep the kit's own frames; the hero's frame has no ground of its own and no light behind it, the page stays white; the main frame's sections stand `clamp(4rem, 8vw, 5rem)` apart; the band is the ink with the canvas's text on it, a dark panel across the page, and on it the accent becomes the band's ink.

- buttons — every button a full pill whatever the palette's corner, in the display face at 0.875rem and bold. The primary is filled with the ink, not the accent, its text the canvas; the secondary is a ghost, transparent with a hairline. In the opening and on the band the primary carries the rainbow edge: a 2px border drawn from a conic gradient of the highlight, the danger, the warning and the accent, turning once every four seconds, still under reduced motion.
- site-header — the bar at 0.875rem; its links pills that take a faint wash of the ink on hover and the current one held; the brand at the display weight, tracked in; today's status a pill on the second ground with a dot of the highlight.
- hero — with no variant, the kit's two halves: the promise on the left, the product's screen on the right. The title at `clamp(2.25rem, 4.6vw, 4.25rem)`, at the display weight, tracked in by 0.04em, at most 18ch; the lead at 1.125rem in the soft ink. The eyebrow a pill tag: the second ground, a hairline, 0.75rem bold, with a dot of the highlight. The facts are stat callouts, the value above its label at the figure size and tracked in, the label at 0.9375rem in the soft ink, with no rule over them. The screen is a picture with a 16px corner, a hairline round it and the one shadow of the page, thrown up and to the left, only in the light.
- marks — hidden everywhere; a head is announced by its eyebrow.
- eyebrows — outside the opening, a meta label: 0.6875rem, uppercase, tracked 0.08em, medium weight, in the soft ink.
- section — the head centred and its title at the heading size, tracked in by 0.035em; a body of words alone centred at 44rem.
- features — tiles in a centred row, three across at most, each a hairline panel with 0.5rem of padding and the picture inset at 16/10; the item title at 1.125rem, bold, tracked in.
- credentials — no strip, no rules: the title a meta label, centred; the logos 2rem high in greyscale at 70%; the names set as grey wordmarks in the display face.
- testimonials — cards on the second ground with no hairline, padded 1.75rem, the opening quotation mark in the highlight.
- process — the steps on a hairline track, each number a pill on the canvas with a hairline, in the ink, uppercase and tracked.
- pricing — plans side by side as hairline panels; the price at the figure size, tracked in; each line of what is included opens on a check in the accent; the plan to take is ringed in the ink rather than the accent, its flag a pill tinted with the highlight in the ink's text.
- jobs, courses, events, faq — tables in one hairline panel, as dense as the product's lists; the facts in a row pills.
- booking, quote, donate — a hairline panel at a form's measure, flat, no light across its top.
- cta-band — the dark panel: the band's own ground across the page, the call centred in one column up to 44rem, the title at 1.25 times the heading size, the lead in the band's soft ink, the actions centred, the details across the foot over a hairline.
- media — a photograph's corner is 16px; with no picture yet, the second ground.
- site-footer — the brand at the display weight; the columns' eyebrows in the ink.
