# ledger

A quiet office of paper and ink: a letterhead in small capitals over a double rule, every section opened by an ink hairline and its number, its head in the margin and its entries in a narrow measure at the right, and every list ruled like a register. Wherever a label meets a figure — the opening's facts, the hours, the firm's details — a dotted leader runs to the figure at the right, as on a statement of account. No cards, no shadows, no marks under the titles.

## Layouts

- journal — the firm told in one measured column, as a letter would be
- split — who they are beside when and where to reach them
- marketing — the long page, kept narrow
- carte — the fields of practice or the fees as the list the page is for
