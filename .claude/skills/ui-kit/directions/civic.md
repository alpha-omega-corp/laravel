# civic

A notice board people trust: a stripe of the accent across the top of the page and again at its foot, the opening one framed notice with the photograph inside it, what is on as rows that each open on a date tile, and the page closed on a solid band asking for the one thing. Large text, generous targets, plain frames.

## Layouts

- marketing — the long page, each notice a section
- board — what is on, how to join and how to help as notices on one board
- split — what it is beside when and where
- carte — the programme or the courses as the list the page is for

## Scheme

`any`

## Looks

The frames: the nav under a 0.375rem stripe of the accent bled across the window, a 2px ink rule under it; the hero on the second ground; the main sections `--section-gap` apart with nothing drawn on the frame itself; the band the strong accent as a surface, where the accent becomes the band's ink — the eyebrow, the mark and the primary button invert, and the secondary is transparent with a 2px edge of that ink; the footer on the second ground between a 2px ink rule and the 0.375rem stripe again. Everywhere: a button in the main, the footer and the header is at least 2.75rem tall with text at 1rem, a secondary button in the main is edged with an inset hairline of the ink, whatever has the focus is ringed in 3px of the accent, and a job, a service, a project or a listing that is a link is ringed in 2px of the accent under the pointer.
