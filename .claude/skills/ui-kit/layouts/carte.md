# carte

No hero: the page opens on what is offered — the menu, the price list or the goods — with the hours
and the way in held beside it while the list scrolls. The list is the page's thesis.

## Shell

`navigation/navbars/*`

## Suits

a restaurant or a café whose carte is the reason to come, a salon's price list, a shop's counter

## Not for

A business with no list — `marketing` or `split`. One whose rooms are the draw — `journal` or
`poster`.

## Regions

What a roll places in each region of this layout's stub, and what each region is.

- nav: the bar across the top — the name, the page's anchors and the one thing to do
- nav — site-header
- lead: a short heading where a hero would be — the name and one line, and nothing to scroll past
- lead — section
- offer: the menu, the price list or the goods, first, in the widest column
- offer — menu | catalogue, catalogue | nothing
- visit: the hours and the way in, beside the offer on a wide screen and held in view while it scrolls; after it on a phone
- visit — schedule, map | nothing
- main: whatever else there is, under the offer
- main — prefabs, features | media | nothing
- band: one band before the footer, for booking or ordering
- band — cta-band | nothing
- footer: contact and links at the foot of the page
- footer — site-footer

## Collapse

The offer, then the visit, then main. The visit column stops being sticky on a phone.

## Beyond the recipe

The offer can carry category anchors above it, sticky with the bar, once it runs past two screens.
A photograph belongs in main or the band, never between the lead and the offer.
