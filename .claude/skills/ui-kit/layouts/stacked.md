# stacked

A top navbar, a page header band, and grids of cards. For dashboards and product
apps.

## Shell

`application-shells/stacked/*`

## Family

`app`

## Suits

dashboards, product apps

## Regions

What a roll places in each region of this layout's stub, and what each region is.

- nav: the bar across the top, full width and unpadded
- nav — navbar
- header: a tinted title band under the bar
- header — page-heading
- main: a grid of cards, three across on a wide screen; every component is one card and nothing spans two
- main — stat + stat + stat, prefabs, stacked-list | feed | description-list, calendar | progress, action-panel | empty-state | alert
