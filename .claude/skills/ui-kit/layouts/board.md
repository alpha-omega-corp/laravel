# board

A board of tiles instead of a stack of sections — the first tile large, the rest one tile each —
under a short opening. Everything the business has to say is visible at once, sized by what matters
most.

## Shell

`navigation/navbars/*`

## Family

`site`

## Suits

a café, a shop, a market stall, a farm with several things going on at once

## Not for

A business with one thing to say — `poster`. One whose list runs long — `carte`, where it has a
column of its own.

## Regions

What a roll places in each region of this layout's stub, and what each region is.

- nav: the bar across the top
- nav — site-header
- hero: a short opening above the board
- hero — hero | section
- main: the board — a grid of tiles three across on a wide screen, the first tile two columns wide and two rows tall, every other one tile; the prefabs are tiles like everything else
- main — features | media | nothing, prefabs, section | nothing, cta-band | nothing
- footer: contact and links at the foot of the page
- footer — site-footer

## Collapse

Two across on a tablet, the first tile still first; one column on a phone, in the order placed.

## Beyond the recipe

As many tiles as there is content, never more: a tile added to fill a gap is filler with a border.
Two or three of them may carry a photograph or a tint; the rest are type.
