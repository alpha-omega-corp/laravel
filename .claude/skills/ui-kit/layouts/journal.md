# journal

A magazine's rhythm — a lead photograph across the page, the story in one narrow column, broken once
by a band of pictures — closing on a colophon rather than a sales band. The page reads like
something written about the place.

## Shell

`navigation/navbars/*`

## Suits

a winery, a chef's restaurant, a farm or a family business with something to tell

## Not for

A business people mostly need to find quickly — `split` or `poster`. One whose list is the draw —
`carte`.

## Regions

What a roll places in each region of this layout's stub, and what each region is.

- nav: the bar across the top — the name and the page's anchors
- nav — site-header
- hero: the lead photograph and the title, across the page
- hero — hero
- story: the opening of the story, in one narrow column set like a page of a magazine
- story — section
- gallery: one band of pictures across the full width, breaking the column once
- gallery — features | media
- main: the practical part in the same narrow column — what it offers, when, and where
- main — prefabs
- footer: the colophon — the name, the contact and the small print
- footer — site-footer

## Collapse

Already one column; the gallery reflows to one picture across.

## Beyond the recipe

This is the layout where a timeline, a pull quote or a dated note belongs, written in the kit's
tokens. Keep the column narrow: a wide section in the story is the magazine turning back into a
landing page.
