# poster

The whole business on one screen — the name large, what it is, and the hours and the way in along
the foot of that screen — with whatever else it has quietly below. The page barely needs to scroll.

## Shell

`navigation/navbars/*`

## Suits

a small café, a food truck, a pop-up, a barber — a place with one thing to say

## Not for

A business with a long list — `carte`. One with a story to tell — `journal`.

## Regions

What a roll places in each region of this layout's stub, and what each region is.

- nav: the bar across the top of the cover
- nav — site-header
- hero: the cover — the name large, what it is and the one thing to do, filling the first screen
- hero — hero
- facts: the foot of that first screen — the hours and the way in, side by side
- facts — schedule, map | nothing
- main: the rest, quietly, below the fold
- main — prefabs, features | section | nothing
- footer: contact and links at the foot of the page
- footer — site-footer

## Collapse

The cover stays one screen tall; the facts stack under the hero inside it and push it taller only
when they must.

## Beyond the recipe

Spend the page's boldness on the cover and keep main plain. A second screen as loud as the first is
two posters, not one.
