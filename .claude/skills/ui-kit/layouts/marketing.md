# marketing

The long page, and the plain one of the site layouts: a bar, a full-width opening, the sections one
under another, a band asking for the one thing, and the footer. For any public site; the other site
layouts are for when the business is shaped a particular way.

## Shell

`navigation/navbars/*`

## Suits

any public site, a landing page, a business with no particular shape

## Not for

A business whose list is why people come — `carte`. One people mostly need to find — `split` or
`poster`. One with a story to tell — `journal`.

## Regions

What a roll places in each region of this layout's stub, and what each region is.

- nav: the bar across the top of every page — the name, the page's anchors and the one thing to do
- nav — site-header
- hero: the opening across the full width, where the page says what it is
- hero — hero
- main: the page's sections, one under another, each far enough from the next to stand on its own
- main — features | nothing, prefabs, features | section | media | nothing
- band: one full-width band before the footer, for the last thing a visitor is asked to do
- band — cta-band | nothing
- footer: contact, hours and links at the foot of the page
- footer — site-footer

## Collapse

One column at every width; nothing reflows.

## Beyond the recipe

The layout the others are measured against, so the one most at risk of the default page: when the
brief has more than one thing to say, give main a second shape — a gallery, a pull quote, a short
list — rather than a second row of cards.
