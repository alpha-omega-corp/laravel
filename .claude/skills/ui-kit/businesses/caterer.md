# caterer

A kitchen that comes to the guests: a caterer, a traiteur, a private chef, an events kitchen, a cook for weddings and company dinners. It is not a restaurant: nobody walks in, there are no opening hours, and every job is priced for its date and its headcount. A visitor wants the formulas and what each costs a head, what a table of theirs looks like, what other hosts said, the answers about tastings and allergies, and a figure for their date.

## Prefabs

- testimonials — hosts, in their words and under their name
- menu — the formulas: the cocktail, the buffet, the seated dinner, each with its price a head
- gallery — tables laid, buffets dressed, the kitchen at work
- faq — tastings, allergies, staff and crockery, how far they travel, the deposit
- quote — a request for a date: the occasion, how many, the budget band, by when

## Directions

- hearth — an evening table: dark and warm, the dishes photographed
- studio — calm and editorial, for a kitchen that sells its plates
- market — the produce first, in the open, for a kitchen that cooks the season
