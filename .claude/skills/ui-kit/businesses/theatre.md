# theatre

A house with a season: a theatre, a cinema, a concert hall, an opera, a cabaret, a puppet stage. It is not a venue, which others hire, nor a band, which travels: the room stays and the programme changes. A visitor wants what is on this week and this season, what a seat or a subscription costs, when the box office answers, what the room is like, and how to reserve.

## Prefabs

- schedule — the box office's hours
- events — the season: each evening with its date, its title and its company
- gallery — the room, the stage, the foyer
- pricing — the seats and the subscriptions
- faq — latecomers, children, access, the cloakroom, exchanges
- booking — a reservation: the evening, how many seats, a word about it
- map — the door, and where to park

## Directions

- hearth — a room at night: dark and warm, the stage lit
- atelier — the season as posters on a white wall
- civic — a public house: the programme and how to come, plainly
