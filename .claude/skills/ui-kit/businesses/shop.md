# shop

A shop with a counter: a boutique, a bookshop, a delicatessen. The site is a window
onto what is inside, and the reason to walk in.

## Prefabs

- catalogue — a preview of what it sells; a preview until /stripe gives it a checkout
- schedule — the opening hours
- map — where it is

## Directions

- counter — a window onto what is inside: the hours and the products first
