# salon

A hairdresser, a barber, a beauty or nail studio. People come for a service at
a price, by appointment, and need to know when there is somebody in.

## Prefabs

- menu — the services and their prices, which is a menu by another name
- schedule — the opening hours
- map — where it is

## Directions

- studio — the work is the loud part: a full-height photograph, a price list, the people
