# hotel

A small hotel or a B&B: guest rooms in a house, on a farm, above a restaurant. A visitor wants to
see the rooms, know what a night costs and what is included, ask for the dates they have in
mind, and find the place after dark.

## Prefabs

- gallery — the rooms, the breakfast table, the view
- menu — the rooms and what a night costs, which is a price list by another name
- faq — arrival and departure, breakfast, parking, pets, cancelling
- booking — a request for a stay: the dates, the guests, the room
- map — the way in, at night

## Directions

- hearth — a house by lamplight: warm rooms photographed in the evening
- studio — calm and full of air: the rooms set like a magazine
