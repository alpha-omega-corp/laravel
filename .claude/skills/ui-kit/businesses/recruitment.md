# recruitment

A firm that puts people into jobs: a recruitment or a temporary work agency, a headhunter, a placement office for one trade. Two visitors read the same site: the candidate, who wants the open positions, and the employer, who wants to know how a search runs and what it costs. It is not a company's own careers page: the list of jobs is the product.

## Prefabs

- testimonials — candidates and employers, in their words and under their name
- jobs — the positions open, each with where and how much of a week
- services — what the firm does for an employer: permanent, temporary, executive search
- process — how a placement runs, from the brief to the first day
- team — the consultants, each with their sector
- booking — a request for a conversation: candidate or employer, the need in a line
- map — the office

## Directions

- launch — one promise and one action each for the candidate and the employer
- ledger — measured and discreet, for a headhunter chosen on standing
- counter — practical and direct: the open positions and the phone first
