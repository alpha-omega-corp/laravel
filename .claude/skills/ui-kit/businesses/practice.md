# practice

A practice of a few people: a physiotherapist, an osteopath, a therapist, a dentist. A visitor
wants to know who will treat them and whether the practice takes their case, what a first visit
is like, when there is somebody in, and how to ask for an appointment.

## Prefabs

- team — the practitioners, each with a photograph and what they treat
- schedule — the practice's hours
- faq — what a first visit asks: insurance, what to bring, cancelling
- booking — an appointment request: the treatment, the day that suits, a word about it
- map — where the practice is, down to the floor and the door

## Directions

- studio — calm and full of air: the people in portraits
- counter — practical: the hours and the way to an appointment first
