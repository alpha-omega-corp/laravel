# festival

A festival, a fair, a market weekend, a race or a tournament: an event that lasts a few days and comes back each year. The site lives for those dates: before, it sells the passes and recruits the volunteers; during, it is the programme in a pocket. A visitor wants the dates and the place at once, who and what is on and when, what a pass costs, how to get there and where to sleep, and how to help.

## Prefabs

- events — the programme, day by day and stage by stage
- team — the line-up: who plays, speaks, cooks or races
- gallery — last year, which is what sells this one
- pricing — the passes: the day, the weekend, the family, the early bird
- faq — getting there, camping, children, what not to bring, refunds
- jobs — the volunteer roles that are open
- map — the ground

## Directions

- launch — one promise, the dates, the passes, a single action
- hearth — a night event: dark and warm, the stage photographed
- trail — an event outdoors: the ground, the season and the weather up front
