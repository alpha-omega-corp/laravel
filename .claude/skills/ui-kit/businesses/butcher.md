# butcher

A butcher's shop or a charcuterie: meat cut to order, the week's specials, a queue at the
counter on Saturday. A visitor wants to know what is in today and what it costs, whether to
order ahead for the weekend, and the answers to what every butcher is asked across the counter.

## Prefabs

- catalogue — the cuts and the specials, with their prices; a preview until /stripe gives it a checkout
- schedule — the hours, early and short on a Saturday
- faq — what is asked over the counter every day: where the meat comes from, ordering for the weekend, the barbecue
- map — where it is, and where to park

## Directions

- counter — the counter itself: the hours as a strip, the cuts in rows
- market — a farm's own butchery: the herd's meat, on a board of glass panels
