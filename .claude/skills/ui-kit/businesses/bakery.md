# bakery

A bakery or a pâtisserie: bread before dawn, cakes for the weekend. A visitor wants to know
what comes out of the oven today and what it costs, whether it is open at seven on a Sunday, and
where to stop the car.

## Prefabs

- catalogue — the breads and the cakes, with prices; a preview until /stripe gives it a checkout
- schedule — the hours, which start before anybody else's
- map — where it is, and where to stop

## Directions

- counter — a counter at seven in the morning: the hours and the bread first
- market — what came out of the oven today, on a board of glass panels
