# winery

A winery or a cellar door: wine from its own vines, sold by the bottle and the case and tasted
on the spot. A visitor wants to know which wines there are and what they cost, what is on — a
tasting, an open cellar, the harvest — when the cellar is open, and how to find a door at the end
of a vineyard track.

## Prefabs

- catalogue — the wines, with their prices; a preview until /stripe gives it a checkout
- events — the tastings, the open-cellar days, the harvest
- gallery — the vines, the cellar and the seasons
- schedule — the cellar door's hours
- map — the way in, which down a vineyard track is rarely where the address says

## Directions

- hearth — a cellar by candlelight: dark and warm, the bottles photographed
- market — the cellar door in summer: this year's bottles on a board of glass panels
