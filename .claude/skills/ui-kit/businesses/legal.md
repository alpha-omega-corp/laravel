# legal

A law firm, a notary, a tax adviser or a fiduciary: people come with a matter they cannot settle alone and choose on standing, not on price. It is not a practice: nobody books a slot by the hour off a list of treatments; they ask for a first meeting about one matter. A visitor wants to know which fields the firm takes, who would handle theirs and what stands behind them, and how a first meeting is arranged and what it costs.

## Prefabs

- services — the fields of practice, each in the client's words
- team — the lawyers, each with their field and their admission
- credentials — the bar, the chambers, the registers the firm is entered in
- faq — what everybody asks before the first meeting: fees, confidentiality, how long
- booking — a request for a first meeting: the matter in a line, a day that suits
- map — the office, which is where the first meeting is

## Directions

- ledger — a quiet office of paper and ink: narrow columns, hairlines, credentials before photographs
- studio — calm and editorial for a small firm that wants its people seen
