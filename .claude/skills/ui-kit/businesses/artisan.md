# artisan

A maker who sells what their hands make: a potter, a jeweller, a cabinetmaker, a leatherworker, a glassblower, a luthier, a weaver. It is not a shop, which resells, nor a trade called out to a house: the pieces are few, each is made, and some are made to order. A visitor wants to see the pieces and what they cost, how one is made and by whom, where the next market or open day is, and how to commission one.

## Prefabs

- catalogue — the pieces for sale, each with its price
- process — how a piece is made, from the material to the finish
- gallery — the workshop, the tools, the pieces in use
- events — the markets, the fairs and the open days
- quote — a commission: the piece, its size, the budget band, by when
- map — the workshop, when it can be visited

## Directions

- atelier — the pieces as plates on a white wall, captions small
- market — a stall in the sun: the pieces first, warm and in the open
- studio — calm and editorial: one piece told at length
