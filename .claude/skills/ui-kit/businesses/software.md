# software

A software product, an app, a platform or a digital service sold by subscription or by licence. Nobody visits in person; the site is the shopfront, the brochure and the sales call. A visitor wants to know in one line what it does, see who already uses it, understand how to start, compare the plans, and ask for a demo. It never takes a payment here: a plan leads to a demo or a sign-up, and a checkout is deployer's /stripe.

## Prefabs

- credentials — who already uses it: the client logos and the certifications
- testimonials — users, in their words and under their name
- process — how to start: from the demo to the first week in production
- pricing — the plans side by side, each with what it includes
- faq — what every buyer asks: data, hosting, support, leaving
- booking — a request for a demo: the team, the need in a line, a day that suits

## Directions

- launch — a product's front page: one promise, proof, plans, a single action
- studio — calm and editorial, for a tool that sells restraint rather than features
