# consultant

One expert or a few, hired for what they know: an independent consultant, a coach, a trainer, an interim manager, a freelance engineer or translator, a small advisory firm. It is not a law firm, whose fields are regulated, nor an agency, which delivers work by a team. A visitor wants to know what problem this person solves and for whom, how an engagement runs and what it costs, who has hired them before, and how to get a first conversation.

## Prefabs

- credentials — who has hired them: clients, certifications, the bodies they belong to
- testimonials — clients, in their words and under their name
- services — what they can be hired for, each in the client's words
- process — how an engagement runs, from the first call to the handover
- faq — rates, availability, confidentiality, remote or on site
- booking — a request for a first conversation: the need in a line, a day that suits

## Directions

- ledger — measured and credentialed: a column of text, hairlines, proof before pictures
- launch — one promise, proof, one action, for a consultant who sells a method
- studio — calm and editorial, for a coach or a trainer who sells a presence
