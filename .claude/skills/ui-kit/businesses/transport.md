# transport

A firm that moves things or people: a carrier, a removal company, a courier, a taxi, a coach or a shuttle service, a logistics depot. It is not a garage, which repairs vehicles: here the vehicle is the tool and the job is a journey priced by distance, volume and date. A visitor wants what the firm carries and where, what it costs, proof it can be trusted with a household or a pallet, and a figure for their own job.

## Prefabs

- services — what is carried: removals, pallets, parcels, people, storage
- credentials — the licences, the insurance and the networks the firm belongs to
- pricing — the rates: by the hour, by the kilometre, by the cubic metre
- faq — insurance, packing, parking permits, delays, payment
- jobs — the positions open: drivers, packers, dispatch
- quote — a request for a figure: what, from where to where, how much of it, by when

## Directions

- yard — a working depot: heavy heads, figures set big, nothing soft
- counter — practical and direct: the phone, the rates and the area first
- launch — one promise and one action, for a service booked from a phone
