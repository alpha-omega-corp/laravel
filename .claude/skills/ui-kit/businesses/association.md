# association

An association, a club, a foundation, a parish, a neighbourhood or a cultural society: it sells nothing and lives on people who come, join, help and give. A visitor wants to know what it does and for whom, what is on and when, who runs it, how to take part as a member or a volunteer, and how to give.

## Prefabs

- events — what is on: the next meetings, outings and evenings
- team — the committee and the people who can be written to
- gallery — what it looks like when it happens
- jobs — what help is wanted: the volunteer roles that are open
- donate — how to give: the association's own giving page and its account
- map — where it meets

## Directions

- civic — a notice board people trust: what is on, how to join and how to help, plainly
- market — warm and in the open, for a club or a neighbourhood society with a lot going on
