# fitness

A yoga, pilates or fitness studio, a dance school, a climbing gym. A visitor wants to know when
it is open, what a membership or a class pass costs, who teaches, and how to try one class
first.

## Prefabs

- schedule — when the studio is open
- menu — the memberships and the class passes with their prices, which is a price list by another name
- team — the teachers and the coaches
- booking — a trial class: the class, the day, a word about the visitor
- map — where it is

## Directions

- studio — calm and full of air: the room photographed, the prices in a narrow column
- counter — bright and practical: the hours and the prices first
