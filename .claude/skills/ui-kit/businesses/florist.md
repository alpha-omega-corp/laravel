# florist

A florist: bouquets for the day, flowers for a wedding or a funeral, plants that last. A visitor
wants to see the work, know what a bouquet costs, and ask for the flowers for a day that
matters.

## Prefabs

- catalogue — the bouquets and the plants, with prices; a preview until /stripe gives it a checkout
- gallery — the work: a wedding, a table, a shop window
- booking — a request for an occasion: the date, the kind of day, a word about it
- schedule — the shop's hours
- map — where the shop is

## Directions

- studio — the flowers are the loud part: full-height photographs and a quiet price list
- counter — a shop with a counter: the hours and the day's bouquets first
- market — a flower stall: the day's bunches, on a board of glass panels in the sun
