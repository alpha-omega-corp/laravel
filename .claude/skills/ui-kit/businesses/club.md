# club

A sports club: football, tennis, gymnastics, sailing, climbing, judo, a rowing or a ski club. It is an association with a calendar: teams, trainings by age and level, matches at the weekend, and sponsors on the fence. A visitor wants which trainings there are for their age and whether there is room, when and where the next matches are, who coaches, what it looks like, and how to come for a trial session.

## Prefabs

- credentials — the sponsors and the federations, by their marks
- courses — the trainings, each with its day, its age group, its price and the places left
- events — the fixtures: the next matches, tournaments and club days
- team — the coaches and the committee
- gallery — the ground, the teams, the season in pictures
- booking — a trial session: the player, the section, a day that suits
- map — the ground

## Directions

- civic — a notice board people trust: the trainings, the fixtures and how to join, plainly
- trail — an outdoor club: the ground and the season up front
- market — warm and in the open, for a village club with a lot going on
