# guide

Someone who takes people outdoors: a mountain guide, a ski or a climbing school, a hiking leader, a kayak or a paragliding outfit, a campsite with a programme. The season, the dates and the conditions decide everything. A visitor wants the outings with their dates, their level, their price and the places left, who will lead them, what it looks like up there, the answers about equipment and weather, and a way to book a place.

## Prefabs

- courses — the outings and the courses, each with its dates, its level, its price and the places left
- faq — equipment, fitness, weather and cancellation, insurance
- team — the guides, each with their licence and their ground
- gallery — the routes, the huts, the summits
- booking — a request for a place or a private day: the outing, how many, a date that suits
- map — the meeting point

## Directions

- trail — the outdoors in season: big landscapes, the dates and the conditions up front
- studio — calm and editorial, for a guide who sells a few long journeys a year
