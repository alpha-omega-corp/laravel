# cafe

A café or a coffee house: breakfast, lunch, a cake in the afternoon, sometimes music at night.
A visitor wants to know whether it is open now, what is on the board and what it costs, and
what is on this week.

## Prefabs

- schedule — the hours, which is what most visits to the site are for
- menu — the drinks and the food, by section, with their prices
- events — the evenings: a concert, a quiz, a reading
- map — where the door is

## Directions

- counter — breakfast and lunch at the counter: bright, the hours and the board first
- studio — a coffee bar of pale wood and air: the cups photographed, the prices in a narrow column
- hearth — a coffee house in the evening: lamplight, the room as the reason to come
