# farm

A farm that sells what it grows: at its own shop, at markets, or by the box. A
visitor wants to know what is in season, when the shop is open and how to get
there down a road with no number on it.

## Prefabs

- catalogue — what is for sale, with prices; a preview until /stripe gives it a checkout
- schedule — when the farm shop and the market stalls are open
- events — the market days, the harvest, the open farm
- map — the way in, which on a farm is rarely where the address says

## Directions

- market — what is in season, on a board of glass panels over a wash of the palette
- counter — a farm shop with a counter: the hours and the produce first
