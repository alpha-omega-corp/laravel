# venue

A place hired for an occasion: a hall, a barn, a château, a loft, a conference centre, a room above a restaurant. People choose it months ahead for a wedding, a seminar or a party, on photographs and on numbers. A visitor wants to see the rooms, know how many each holds and what comes with it, read what others made of their day there, and ask whether a date is free and what it would cost.

## Prefabs

- listings — the rooms, each with its capacity, its surface and what it is for
- gallery — the place dressed for a wedding, a dinner, a seminar
- testimonials — hosts, in their words and under their name
- faq — what every host asks: caterers, music and curfew, parking, the deposit
- quote — a request for a date: the occasion, how many, the budget band, by when
- map — how guests get there

## Directions

- studio — calm and editorial: the rooms photographed with air around them
- hearth — an evening place: dark and warm, the room lit for a dinner
- atelier — the rooms as plates on a white wall, for a venue that is architecture first
