# trades

A trade: a plumber, an electrician, a carpenter, a roofer. Nobody walks in; people call when
something breaks or when a job is planned. A visitor wants to see work like theirs done well,
know the area served and what a visit costs, and ask for a quote without a phone call.

## Prefabs

- gallery — jobs done, before and after
- faq — what everybody asks first: the area served, the call-out charge, emergencies, the guarantee
- booking — a request for a visit or a quote: the job, the day that suits, a word about it
- map — the workshop, which is the middle of the area served

## Directions

- counter — practical and direct: the phone, the area and the work up front
- studio — the work is the loud part: joinery and finishes photographed with air around them
