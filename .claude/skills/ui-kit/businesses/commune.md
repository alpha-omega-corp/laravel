# commune

A public body people have to deal with: a municipality, a commune, a district office, a public utility, a school authority. It sells nothing and cannot be chosen; what it owes a visitor is the answer in two clicks. A visitor wants the counter's hours and where it is, which service handles their matter and what to bring, what is on in the village, who sits on the council, and which posts are open.

## Prefabs

- schedule — the counter's hours, department by department when they differ
- services — what the administration does for a resident: papers, permits, waste, school
- faq — what to bring, what it costs, how long it takes
- events — the agenda: council sittings, markets, collections, celebrations
- team — the council and the heads of service
- jobs — the posts that are open
- map — the town hall

## Directions

- civic — a notice board people trust: plain, legible, the answer first
- ledger — measured and official: columns, hairlines, nothing decorative
