# rental

A business that lends for a price: cars and vans, bikes and skis, boats, tools and machines, party tents and crockery. It is not a shop, which sells, nor a garage: what leaves comes back, so everything turns on dates, deposits and conditions. A visitor wants what can be rented with the facts that matter, what a day or a week costs, the rules on deposit, licence and insurance, when the counter is open, and a booking for their dates.

## Prefabs

- schedule — the counter's hours: when to collect and when to bring back
- listings — what can be rented, each with the facts it is chosen by
- pricing — the rates: the half day, the day, the weekend, the week
- faq — the deposit, the licence, insurance, late returns, damage
- booking — a request for dates: what, from when to when, how many
- map — where to collect

## Directions

- counter — practical and direct: what there is, the rates and the hours first
- trail — rentals outdoors: skis, bikes and boats, the season up front
- yard — machines and vans: heavy heads, figures set big
