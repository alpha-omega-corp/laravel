# construction

A builder: a construction company, a civil engineering contractor, a firm that puts up halls, houses and bridges. Nobody walks in; a client arrives with a plot, a plan or a problem and a budget in the millions or the thousands. A visitor wants to see jobs like theirs finished, know what the firm builds itself and what stands behind it, understand how a job runs, and ask for a figure without a phone call.

## Prefabs

- services — what the firm builds itself: structures, civil works, renovation, studies
- projects — jobs done, each with where, when and the one figure that sizes it
- credentials — the certifications and memberships a client's tender asks for
- process — how a job runs, from the first visit to the handover
- jobs — the positions open on site and in the office
- quote — a request for a figure: the kind of work, where, the budget band, by when
- map — the yard and the office, which is where a client comes to sign

## Directions

- yard — an industrial site in daylight: heavy heads, big figures, a hazard band, the work up front
- counter — practical and direct for a smaller builder: the phone, the area and the jobs first
