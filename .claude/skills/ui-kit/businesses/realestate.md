# realestate

An estate agency, a property manager, a broker: flats and houses to let and to sell, one object at a time, until each is gone. Buyers and tenants come for the listings; owners come to know whether this agency should sell theirs. A visitor wants what is on the market now with its rooms, its surface and its price, proof the agency sells in their street, and a way to ask for a viewing or a valuation.

## Prefabs

- listings — what is on the market, each with its facts, its price and where it stands
- services — what the agency does for an owner: selling, letting, managing, valuing
- testimonials — owners and buyers, in their words and under their name
- team — the brokers, each with their area
- booking — a request for a viewing or a valuation: the object, a day that suits
- map — the agency, in the middle of the streets it sells

## Directions

- counter — the listings first: practical, the phone and today's new objects up front
- atelier — properties shown as plates, for an agency that sells houses worth looking at
- studio — calm and editorial, for a boutique agency with a few objects at a time
