# spa

A place people come to rest: a spa, thermal baths, a hammam, a sauna, a massage or a wellness centre. It is not a salon, where a service is done and the client leaves, nor a fitness studio: the place itself is half of what is bought. A visitor wants the treatments and what they cost, the entry passes, what the rooms and the water look like, when it is open and how busy, and a slot on Saturday.

## Prefabs

- schedule — the opening hours, the quiet hours and the mixed ones
- menu — the treatments, by kind, each with its length and its price
- pricing — the entry: two hours, the day, the evening, ten entries
- faq — what to bring, mixed and textile rules, children, gift cards
- gallery — the baths, the cabins, the garden
- booking — a request for a slot: the treatment, the day, how many
- map — the door, and where to park

## Directions

- studio — calm and editorial: the rooms photographed with air around them
- hearth — an evening place: dark and warm, the water lit
- trail — baths outdoors: the landscape and the season first
