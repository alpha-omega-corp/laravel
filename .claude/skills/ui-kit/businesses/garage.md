# garage

A car garage, a body shop, a tyre fitter, a motorcycle or a bicycle workshop: people bring a vehicle when something is wrong or when a service is due, and some come to buy one. A visitor wants to know whether it is open, what a service costs, whether the workshop can be trusted with their car, which used vehicles are for sale, and how to get a slot this week.

## Prefabs

- schedule — the workshop's hours, which decide when the car can be dropped off
- menu — the price list: services, tyres, bodywork, each with what it costs
- listings — the vehicles for sale, each with its year, its mileage and its price
- testimonials — customers, in their words and under their name
- booking — a request for a slot: the vehicle, the job, a day that suits
- map — where to bring the car

## Directions

- yard — a working shop: heavy heads, prices and plates set like figures, nothing soft
- counter — practical and direct: the hours, the price list and the phone first
