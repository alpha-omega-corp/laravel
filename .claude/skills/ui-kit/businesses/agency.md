# agency

A team that makes things for clients: a web, a design, a communication, an advertising or a video agency, a studio of a few people or of fifty. It is not a portfolio, which is one person, nor a software product, which is sold as it is. A visitor wants to see work done for businesses like theirs, know what the agency actually does itself and how a project runs, meet the people, and send a brief.

## Prefabs

- credentials — the clients, by their marks
- testimonials — clients, in their words and under their name
- projects — the work, each with for whom, when and the one figure that says it worked
- services — what the agency does itself: strategy, design, build, content
- process — how a project runs, from the brief to the launch
- team — the people a client will work with
- quote — a brief: the kind of project, the budget band, by when

## Directions

- launch — a product's front page: one promise, the proof, a single action
- atelier — the work on white walls: case studies as plates
- studio — calm and editorial: one project told at length
