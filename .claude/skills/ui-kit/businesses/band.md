# band

People who play: a band, a soloist, an ensemble, a choir, a DJ, a theatre company on tour. Two audiences read the same site: those who want to come and hear, and those who want to hire. A visitor wants the next dates and where to get in, the records and where to listen, who plays what, what a night looks like, and a way to book the act for an evening.

## Prefabs

- events — the next concerts, each with its date, its town and its room
- catalogue — the records and what else is sold at the merch table
- team — who plays what
- gallery — the stage, the room, the road
- booking — a request to hire: the occasion, the date, the room and its size

## Directions

- hearth — a room at night: dark and warm, the stage photographed, the dates in the accent
- atelier — the records as plates on a white wall, for an ensemble whose covers are half the work
