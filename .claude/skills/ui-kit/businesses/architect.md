# architect

An architecture practice, an interior architect, a landscape or an engineering studio that designs what others build. Clients choose on the work and on whether they can talk to the person who drew it. A visitor wants to look at built projects like theirs, understand how a commission runs from the first sketch to the keys, meet the people, and ask for a first conversation about a plot or a house.

## Prefabs

- projects — built work, each with where, when and its surface
- process — how a commission runs: sketch, permit, tender, site, handover
- team — the partners and the people who draw
- credentials — the registers and the prizes: the profession's own proof
- quote — a request for a first conversation: the kind of project, where, the budget band
- map — the studio

## Directions

- atelier — work on white walls: the projects as plates, captions small, the person last
- studio — calm and editorial: one project told at length, a lot of air
