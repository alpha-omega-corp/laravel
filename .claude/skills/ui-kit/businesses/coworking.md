# coworking

A shared workspace: desks by the day or the month, closed offices, meeting rooms by the hour. People compare plans and want to see the room before they commit to a month. A visitor wants the plans side by side with what each includes, the space in pictures, what happens there, the answers about access and notice, and a way to book a visit or a trial day.

## Prefabs

- services — what every member gets: the desks, the rooms, the network, the coffee
- pricing — the plans side by side: the day pass, the flex desk, the fixed desk, the office
- faq — access hours, notice, guests, the address for mail
- gallery — the room, the meeting rooms, the terrace
- events — what happens there: breakfasts, talks, open days
- booking — a visit or a trial day: a day that suits, what they are looking for
- map — where it is, and how far from the station

## Directions

- launch — a product's front page: one promise, the plans, one action
- studio — calm and editorial, for a space that sells its light and its quiet
- spec — a space run like a product: the plans and the bookings as dense as an app, one action
