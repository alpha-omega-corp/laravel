# school

A school that is chosen: a language school, a music school, a tutor, a driving school, a training centre, a nursery. Parents and adults compare courses, dates and people before they enrol. A visitor wants the courses with their dates, their level, their price and the places left, who teaches, the answers every parent asks for, and a way to enrol or to ask for a trial lesson.

## Prefabs

- courses — the sessions, each with its dates, its level, its price and the places left
- team — the teachers, each with what they teach
- faq — what everybody asks first: levels, absences, payment, certificates
- booking — an enrolment or a trial lesson: the course, the learner, a day that suits
- map — where the lessons are

## Directions

- civic — a notice board people trust: plain, legible, the programme and how to join up front
- studio — calm and editorial, for a music or an art school that shows its rooms
- counter — practical and direct, for a tutor or a driving school: the dates, the prices, the phone
