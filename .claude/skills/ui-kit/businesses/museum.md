# museum

A museum, an art gallery, an exhibition space, a heritage site or a visitor centre: a place people come to look, for an hour or an afternoon. It is not a venue, which is hired, nor a theatre, which sells an evening. A visitor wants to know what is on show now and until when, whether it is open today and what the ticket costs, what the permanent collection holds, and how to get there with a pushchair or a class of twenty.

## Prefabs

- schedule — the opening hours, late evenings and closed days included
- events — the exhibitions and what else is on: guided tours, openings, workshops
- gallery — the collection and the rooms, in the order the museum shows them
- pricing — admission: the full ticket, the reduced one, families, the annual pass
- faq — access, bags and photographs, groups and school classes
- map — the entrance, and how to reach it

## Directions

- atelier — work on white walls: the exhibits as plates, captions set like a museum label
- civic — a public house: plain, legible, what is on and how to visit said at once
- studio — calm and editorial, for a small gallery with one exhibition at a time
