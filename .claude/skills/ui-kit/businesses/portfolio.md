# portfolio

One person's work shown to be hired: a photographer, an illustrator, a filmmaker, a graphic or a product designer. The pictures are the argument and everything else stays out of their way. A visitor wants to look at the work, see whole series rather than single shots, know what can be commissioned and roughly what it costs, and write to the person who made it.

## Prefabs

- gallery — the work, in the order its author puts it
- projects — the series and the commissions, each with for whom and when
- services — what can be commissioned: a wedding, a portrait, a campaign
- testimonials — clients, in their words and under their name
- booking — a request for a date or a commission: the occasion, the day, a word about it

## Directions

- atelier — work on white walls: plates in a strict grid, captions small, the person last
- studio — calm and editorial: one series told at length, a lot of air
