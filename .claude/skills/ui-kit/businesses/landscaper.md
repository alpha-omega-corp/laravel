# landscaper

Someone who makes and keeps gardens: a landscape gardener, a paysagiste, a tree surgeon, a nursery that plants what it grows, a firm that lays terraces and pools. It is a trade, but one whose work is looked at for years and changes with the season. A visitor wants gardens like theirs done well, what the firm does itself from the design to the upkeep, what clients said two summers later, and a visit and a figure.

## Prefabs

- services — what the firm does itself: design, planting, paving, pruning, upkeep
- testimonials — clients, in their words and under their name
- projects — gardens made, each with where, when and its surface
- faq — the right season, upkeep contracts, permits for trees, the guarantee on plants
- quote — a request for a visit and a figure: the garden, the work, the budget band
- map — the yard and the nursery

## Directions

- trail — the outdoors in season: big photographs of gardens, the time of year up front
- market — warm and in the open: the plants and the produce first
- yard — a working firm: machines, terraces and walls, figures set big
