# garage

Twelve ways one garage is drawn, seven as a working yard and five as a plain counter. The pages and the prefabs are the same in all of them; what changes is what the first screen leads with — the hours and the door, the price list, the forecourt or the finish of the work — and how the prices and the stock are read: in rows beside the hours, as tiles on a board, or led by one vehicle drawn large.

## Atelier du village

The independent all-makes workshop most people mean when they say the garage: the name and what it does on the first screen, this week's hours and the way in held beside it, then the price list down one long page and the few cars for sale as tiles on a board. For a village or small-town garage whose customers ask three things, whether it is open, what a service costs and when they can drop the car off.
