# recruitment

Twelve ways one recruitment firm is drawn: five as launch's promise, four on ledger's paper and three at counter's desk. The pages and the prefabs are the same in all of them; what changes is which of its two visitors the first screen speaks to — the candidate who wants the open positions or the employer who wants to know how a search runs and what it costs — and whether the firm is looked up on a phone at a factory gate or studied for a week by a board before anybody calls.

## Agence régionale

The regional agency most people mean by the word: a handful of consultants, permanent and temporary work, industry first. The first screen is the promise set large with one action for each visitor, the open positions for the candidate and the brief for the employer, and the long page under it carries the two doors, the proof and the office on its map. The positions and the consultants are boards, a tile each, because both are scanned for one's own trade; what the firm does for an employer is five tiles and four steps joined in a row, read in one pass down the long page, and the request for a conversation stands beside the way in. Graphite's instrument-panel blue for a firm that places into precision workshops.

## Profils numériques

A young firm that places developers and data people and has no office worth photographing: the first screen is one sentence set large on a night sky, with no picture, and the action under it. For a candidate who reads it at eleven at night and is courted by four other recruiters that week, so each position is a card with its paragraph rather than a row of facts, each recruiter is a row saying what they hire for, and the form is bare on the page. What the firm sells to an employer is a board, glanced at between two meetings. Nocturne pinned dark, since that is the screen its readers already live in.

## Relève soignante

Staffing for care homes, home-care services and practices: the promise beside a photograph of the work, and beside both the office and its map with nothing drawn round it, because a nurse between two shifts wants to know where to walk in and whether there is a post this week. Everything after home is a board, the posts, the offer to a head of care and the consultants, visible at once on a phone in a staff room and sized by what matters. One placed nurse's sentence is set large over the other voices. Lagoon pinned light: calm water and daylight, never a ward at night.

## Premier emploi

The desk for school-leavers and apprentices finishing their certificate, who have never been inside an agency and expect to be turned away. The first screen is a photograph of people their age at work with the promise over it and one button, under the name centred over the pages; the positions are a board of tiles, the voices of those placed last year flow down a wall, the road for an employer is four steps in a row, and the request for a first conversation is an invitation on one side and a short form on the other. Sorbet is only fair on launch, stickers and peach paper under a product's promise, and is chosen for exactly that: this is the one audience an agency may greet like a Saturday.

## Forfait PME

The firm that sells on price to small companies with no personnel department: its visitor is an owner with two other agencies' quotes open beside it. Home is the long page with the promise centred over the workshop and the streets round the office drawn wide; on the employers' board the first tile is the services ruled as rows, the figure at the end of each line, with the steps down one rule beside them, each with how long it takes. The positions are kept to a plain page and the consultants to a board, since neither is what this visitor came to compare. Denim, sturdy and plain-spoken, for a reader who distrusts gloss.

## Chasseur de têtes

A headhunter chosen on standing, by a board that reads the site twice and calls rather than fill in a form. The first screen is one sentence with no photograph, under the name centred like a letterhead; home, the method and the consultants are each told in one measured column, as a letter would be, with a client's sentence set large before the others and the road of a mandate down one rule. The few mandates that may be published are a list read down like a register, and each consultant is a row with what they did before. Vellum's black on white with one red: nothing on the page is louder than the name.

## Vieille maison

The placement office that has been on the same street for a generation and is known in town by its founder's name. The first screen is two halves, who they are beside where to walk in, the map with no panel round it; the positions and then the fees are each the list their page is for, ruled as rows with the figure at the end, because the employers who come here have come before and want the tariff rather than the pitch. Each consultant is a row with the trade they came from, the form is bare beside the way in, and the page closes on one centred column. Quill's bone paper and bound-volume navy for a house whose argument is that it is still there.

## Finance et droit

A specialist boutique placing accountants, fiduciaries, paralegals and compliance officers, read by candidates at their desk in the firm they mean to leave. Home is the long page kept narrow, the words centred over one wide photograph, short voices in a wall and the streets round the door drawn across the page; each position is a card with its paragraph, since a senior accountant wants the scope before the place; the fees are the list their page is for; and the consultants are told in a column, because here the person is the product. The form is bare on the page. Harbour, cool and dense, for people who read figures all day.

## Mandats confidentiels

Discreet searches for family-owned companies and foundations replacing a director nobody yet knows is leaving. The first screen is a photograph behind one sentence, on aubergine at night; home and the consultants are letters in one column, and no list leads anything: the open mandates are a plain page, deliberately quiet. The offer is five tiles and four steps in a row on the long employers' page, and the request for a conversation is an invitation on one side and the form on the other. Plum is only fair on ledger, a little theatrical for an office of paper, and it is pinned dark here on purpose: this site is a closed door and should look like one.

## Saisonniers

Seasonal placement for hotels, mountain restaurants and the vineyards: the same people come back each November and each May, and the first screen greets them with the words centred over a wide photograph, the office and its map beside it, under the name centred over the pages. One returning worker's sentence is set large over the other voices. The positions and the offer to a hotel keeper are long plain pages read on a phone in a staff dormitory, the offer five tiles with an icon each; the consultants are a board; the page closes on one centred column. Orchard's deep green on warm paper, drawn rather than rendered, for work that follows the seasons.
