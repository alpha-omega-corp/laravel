# school

Twelve ways one school is drawn, across the three looks it may take: five notice boards, four studios and three counters. The pages and the prefabs are the same in all of them; what changes is what the first screen leads with (the door, the season, a room or one sentence), how the courses are read (dated rows from the top of their page, or a card each), and whether the teachers are notices on a board, a story or a list of people with something to say.

## École du quartier

The school most towns have, a music or a language school that parents compare with the one in the next town: the name beside the map on the first screen, the courses in rows from the top of their page with their dates, their prices and the places left, and the teachers as notices on one board. For a school whose visitors ask two things first, what starts when and where it is, and then want the form beside a sentence that says the first lesson is free.

## Programme annuel

An association's school or an adult-education programme whose season is too long to hold in the head, a language school with six levels or a people's university: the long notice-board page under an opening centred on one wide photograph, the map across the page, and the questions in two columns because there are many and they are read at a desk. Enrolling is a page that opens on its heading and goes straight to the form. For the school a visitor studies for a week before choosing a course.

## Stages de saison

A ski school, a swimming school or a holiday camp: the sessions are weeks, they fill up, and the visitor is standing in the resort with a phone. The first screen is the photograph of the place with the name on it, beside the map to the meeting point; each week is a card with its places left, and the instructors are a board. Snow, lake blue and a rescue orange that makes the last places read from across the street. For a school that sells a season, in the country or on the mountain.

## Petite enfance

A nursery, a playgroup or a pre-school that parents study for a week before they visit: the rooms and the people come before any price. The opening sets the words beside a photograph of the room, the map runs across the home page because the daily journey decides as much as anything, the groups are a few cards that each deserve a look, and every answer on the questions page is already open. Calm aqua and round type, pinned light: a page about small children's rooms should look like daylight whatever the phone is set to.

## Ateliers du mercredi

Children's workshops after school, circus, robotics, pottery or theatre, that want to look as loud as they sound: the first screen is one large sentence beside the map, with no photograph to wait for, then the dated rows. Marigold is only fair on a notice board, which it makes shout, and that is the point here. The leaders are a list, each with what they run, and the questions, many and practical (what to bring, who may collect a child), sit in two columns. For a young association run by the people who lead the workshops.

## Salle de musique

A music school that shows its rooms: the cover is one photograph and the name, the way in along its foot, and the courses are a quiet narrow column read like a price list. The teachers and the questions are set like pages of a magazine, the rooms across them. For a school chosen for its rooms and its instruments, by adults as much as by parents, that sells on craft and never on a discount.

For a newcomer that wants to look generous rather than serious.

## Scène de nuit

A jazz, a rock or a theatre school that teaches after work and plays out at night: pinned dark, on the violet palette that turns into a night sky. Nocturne is only fair on this look, electric where the studio is quiet, and right for a school whose best room is a stage.

## Vieille académie

The questions and the enrolment are plain long pages that close on the same quiet ask.

The few questions everybody asks are all open, and the form stands on the page with no panel round it. Yellow and black, loud and practical. For the school that sells on the next date and the price, and answers the phone.
