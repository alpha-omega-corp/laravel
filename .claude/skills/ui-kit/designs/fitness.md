# fitness

Fourteen ways one fitness studio is drawn: seven as a calm studio, seven as a bright counter. The pages and the prefabs are the same in all of them; what changes is what the visitor is taken to have come for (the room, a teacher, the price of a month, whether it is open tonight), and so what the first screen leads with and how the prices are read: as a list with dotted leaders, as a few cards, or as sections side by side.

## Studio de yoga

The yoga and pilates studio most people mean by the word: a long first page that opens on the room photographed with air around it, then the three doors (prices, teachers, the trial class), the week and a wide map of the courtyard it hides in. The prices are a list with dotted leaders from the top of their page, the teachers a story broken once by pictures, and the trial class an invitation on one side and the form on the other. For a town studio with forty classes a week that people compare with two others before they try one.

## Atelier pilates

Two teachers, eight machines and no crowd to photograph: the cover is the teacher's portrait beside the words, with the week and the door at the foot of the same screen and nothing loud under it. The few formulas are cards worth reading one by one, the teachers are told as a story and then listed as rows with what each one does, and the trial form sits on the page with no panel round it. For a pilates or personal-training studio that sells on craft, on warm printed paper, where the visitor reads for a week before trusting somebody with their back.

## Entraînement médical

A training room attached to a physiotherapy practice: machines, small groups after an injury, a subscription the health insurer partly refunds. The visitor has a prescription in hand and wants to know when it is open and what is covered, so the first screen sets the name beside this week's hours and the door, the prices are a list in rows from the top of their page with the refund on each line, the therapists a list with their qualifications, and the trial session is the long page with the form on it. Harbour is good at the counter: cool blue-grey and hairlines, which read as care rather than as a gym.
